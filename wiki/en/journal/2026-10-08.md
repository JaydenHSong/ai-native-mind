## Insights

"**The OS became the third home**": local (privacy) and cloud (workflow) are joined by OS-embedded (platform) — Surface Ultra + Agentic Windows hardens agent security into an OS-level standard (Containment, Identity, Manageability). The [[concepts/agent-residence]] 3-axis map is complete.

"**Price is strategy**": Haiku 5.5's 90% cut and DeepSeek's $12B raise landed in the same week — one is the routing economics of "the right model per job," the other is "unit-economics advantage attracts capital." [[patterns/mid-tier-performance-inversion]] and [[comparisons/frontier-lab-economics]] are two sides of the same coin.

"**Peak verification asymmetry**": 722 manuscripts released while the model, prompts, and Issues stay closed. Results open under Apache-2.0, process fully closed — and mathematicians answer with "receipts." Verification asymmetry is now the canonical case in [[patterns/agent-scientific-discovery]].

"**Decision models, four in 11 days**": Jev → Clef/Decider 2B → Decisions API → PolicyLM-1.7B. The hybrid architecture that moves repetitive judgments off generative LLMs is becoming the practical standard — a candidate "judgment layer" for agent guardrails.
