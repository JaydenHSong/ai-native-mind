# 2026-10-18 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **DeepSeek V4.1-Flash-1017** (10/17): MIT open weights released (763B MoE, 16B active). Terminal-Bench 4.0 31.2 → 42.3, DeepSWE v1.1 77.0 — vendor-measured. Per-task cost under $0.2; API output $0.6/1M tokens off-peak, ~1/42 of Claude Opus 5. New Search/Fetch tool APIs ($0.75/1,000 calls). HF CSO Thomas Wolf: curious whether the numbers hold outside internal evaluation.
- **IMF Asia-Pacific Regional Economic Outlook** (10/18): ~40M job postings across 19 economies — demand in AI-exposed occupations fell ~10% since ChatGPT's launch, stabilizing. New demand for prompt engineering, RAG, AI agent development. Only ~6% of firms explicitly request AI skills.
- **Anthropic's Jack Clark on $44T GDP** (10/17, Bangkok IMF–World Bank meetings): cited an internal economic model for 2030 US GDP of ~$44T (now $30T+); acknowledged it "sounds very outlandish." The $44T is the model's extreme scenario (+32.4%), not an official forecast. Called for "unusual and serious rules."
- **Anthropic AI safety bill draft** (originally reported 10/16, re-reported 10/18): privately lobbying Congress. Shutdown only on "foreseeable, imminent risk" of $1B+ damage or 50+ deaths/serious injuries, via Commerce Department → DC federal judge. Risk pathways limited to bioweapons, control escape, cyberattacks. Not a bill yet.
- **OpenCode key exposure** (10/17–18): decommissioned legacy console breached, potential leak of OpenAI/Anthropic/Google provider keys. ~1.4% of Console users. Two misuse reports (~$100 in fraudulent charges). Email-only notification, no status-page post. Company cites a "distillation attack."
- **Platforms blocking agents** (10/17, The Verge): Yelp (paid data licensing only), Delta (no partnership), JPMorgan and Schwab (no credential-using agents). Amazon vs Perplexity suit swung in August (appeal reversed the ban). Meta's counter: "turning away a personal agent means turning away the customer behind it" — partnerships with Walmart, Gap, Sephora, Best Buy.

## Insight

Today's news converges on one line: **"The agents knocked, and the doors started answering."** Yesterday's news was "how far agents got in" (Tencent's 2,818 attempts, CERT-In intrusions, the Sepolia wallet); today is **"how far they can't get in."** Yelp draws the line with money (licensing), banks with identity (credentials), Anthropic with law (shutdown procedure), and dev tools with key hygiene (API keys). DeepSeek's 1/42 price adds the paradox — entry gets cheaper while entry points shrink. The practical conclusion for harness builders: draw the **access map** (where is blocked) and the **cost map** (where is cheap) separately.
