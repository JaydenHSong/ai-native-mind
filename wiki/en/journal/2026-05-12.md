# 2026-05-12 Learning Journal — preparation · reliability · grounding

## Start here

The shared idea was that model capability is nearing a ceiling, so performance increasingly depends on lower layers.

## Three levers

- **Preparation** — what should be finished before coding starts so the agent is aligned with the task?
- **Reliability** — how trustworthy is the judge or evaluator loop?
- **Grounding** — how explicitly is the agent tied to evidence and scope?

## What changed in the wiki

- Strengthened [[concepts/context-engineering]].
- Strengthened [[concepts/llm-evaluation]].
- Strengthened [[patterns/claude-md-guide]].
- Reconnected all three to [[concepts/harness-engineering]].

## Why it mattered

This day sharpened the wiki’s habit of asking where performance differences come from when the model is no longer the obvious explanation. The answer increasingly lay in preparation, judging, and epistemic grounding.

## Follow-up

- Keep treating context preparation as a first-class engineering lever.
- Keep separating evaluator reliability from raw model output quality.
