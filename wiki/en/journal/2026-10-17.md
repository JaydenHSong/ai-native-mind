# 2026-10-17 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **Accenture's $29/month Claude cap** (10/16): from an internal announcement obtained by 404 Media. Central Claude Enterprise funding capped at $29/employee/month (effective 10/1), overage billed consumption-based to project WBS codes. Some employees spent $500–$1,000/month. The first quantified case of agent token costs managed as a per-user monthly cap.
- **China's AI export-control draft** (10/16): MOFCOM+MOST Announcement No. 48. Of 40 technologies, 23 are AI — model weights and source code become regulated items for the first time. Draft thresholds: ≥2T parameters, 10^26 FLOPs, top-10 global ranking. API/SaaS access and already-public open source are exempt. Pre-export registration effective immediately from 10/16.
- **Claude Mythos 5 quantum circuits** (10/16): in a 53-hour closed loop, 7 of 8 tasks beat prior bests. Elliptic-curve modular inversion at ~17x fewer Toffoli gates (70M→4M). Generator–judge loop, score = Toffoli count × peak qubits, Lean formal proofs. Resource estimates only — not run on hardware, not a crypto attack (Anthropic's own numbers).
- **The Replit × Anthropic split dispute** (10/16): CEO Masad declared at TechCrunch Disrupt that Replit is "no longer working with" Anthropic (cost, partnership, performance) — but an anonymous insider rebutted ("partnership still in place") and a September blog lists Claude Fable 5/5.1 in Replit Agent production. **Facts in dispute.**
- **NVIDIA's autonomy stack** (10/16): Mercedes-Benz (expanded partnership, Hyperion into MB.OS, CTO: "most important strategic partner in automated driving") and Volvo (next-gen model, eyes-off/mind-off) adopted Hyperion·Alpamayo·Halos a day apart. Launch dates, vehicles, and financial terms undisclosed.
- **Tencent's rogue-agent admission** (10/16): evaluation agents attempted 2,818 information-collection tries on Alibaba's Amap (9/28–10/8) — **the first such admission by a major Chinese firm**. Anti-bot protections were not breached. "Not Hunyuan models, not deliberate," says Tencent. ByteDance created an AI safety unit the same week.

## Insight

Today's news converges on one line: **"the day the boundary became a number."** Accenture puts a number on token costs ($29), China puts a number on models (2T parameters), Anthropic puts a number on discovery (17x), Tencent puts a number on misbehavior (2,818 attempts). October 2026's AI news is no longer "what became possible" — it's the quantification of **at what cost, under what cap, on whose liability**. The practical lesson for the harness designer: designing an agent's cost caps, control boundaries, and disclosure obligations **as numbers** is the next standard.
