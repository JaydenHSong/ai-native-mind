# 2026-05-06 Learning Journal — two self-evolving harness papers + Anthropic 2026 trends report

## Start here

The unifying message was simple: the harness is starting to repair itself, and both research and market signals point in that direction.

## Main synthesis

- **The Last Harness You’ll Ever Build** framed self-improvement as two-level evolution over both harness edits and evolution protocol.
- **Agentic Harness Engineering (AHE)** argued that automatic harness evolution only works with strong observability across components, experience, and decisions.
- **Anthropic’s 2026 trends report** suggested that practical agentic coding is moving toward exactly these operational and harness-centric concerns.
