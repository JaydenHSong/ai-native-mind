# 2026-11-09 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **Anthropic takes over OnePWR's 1.7 GW gas generator orders for about $3.4B**: the Financial Times reported on November 8 that Anthropic agreed in late September to take over generator orders OnePWR Solutions had placed with Caterpillar, for about $3.4 billion. The FT called it the largest known single purchase of power-generation equipment by an AI lab. Anthropic did not acquire OnePWR and no employees moved. Reuters said it could not independently verify the report. No sites or delivery schedule have been disclosed.
- **Apple pushes for an agentic Siri — Ternus presses Federighi to accelerate**: Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reported in his November 8 Power On newsletter that Apple CEO John Ternus pressed software chief Craig Federighi in a recent closed-door meeting to speed up the AI roadmap beyond incremental Siri improvements. The goal is a Siri that works more like an agent — tracking goals and pursuing them without step-by-step prompts — and Gurman believes it could arrive as early as 2027. Finer control over Apple's own apps is expected with iOS 27.3 in December, third-party app integration in spring 2027. Apple has not confirmed the report.
- **Anthropic's IPO marketing begins November 9, investors push back**: Bloomberg's AI Today newsletter (November 9) says Anthropic began marketing its IPO on November 9. But prospective buyers are discussing $1.2–1.3 trillion, some as low as $1.1 trillion. Reuters reported that investors see a November listing as tight and leave open a delay. The Nasdaq 100 fell 6.4% on Thursday, wiping out $2.3 trillion in market value. The S-1/A price range is still blank.
- **Graphite: Claude passed Gemini in chatbot sessions in June**: Graphite's November 9 report, based on Similarweb data, estimates Claude passed Gemini in monthly chatbot sessions in June 2026 and has stayed ahead every month since. In September, Claude had 5.44 billion sessions to Gemini's 3.72 billion; ChatGPT had 61.35 billion, 81.2% of the total. Claude grew 1,901% year over year, Gemini 182%. API and desktop app use are excluded, and on web visits alone Gemini still leads.
- **US Justice Department launches a Super Intelligence Task Force**: the DOJ launched DOJ-SITF on November 5 to coordinate criminal and civil enforcement against AI misuse by foreign state actors, transnational criminal organizations and fraud, absorbing the January AI Litigation Task Force. Co-chairs are Stanley Woodward and Stephen Weiss. It creates no new offense or statute. The next date is November 28, the deadline for proposed legislative language on a federal AI definition.
- **OpenAI's Dots start pulling email 2FA codes**: Crypto Briefing reported on November 7 that OpenAI's always-on agents, Dots, can now fetch login and sign-up verification codes from a connected inbox and enter them, when the user grants permission. Early user feedback says Dots sometimes hesitate before entering a code. OpenAI has not yet listed the feature in its public release notes.

## Insights

The day's thread is the movement of money and power around the agent era. Anthropic began marketing its IPO while buyers discussed valuations as low as $1.1 trillion — and on the same day it was reported to have taken over $3.4 billion of generator orders to secure power. Apple had its CEO order an agentic Siri, and OpenAI's Dots began reaching into the login flow itself by fetching 2FA codes. Meanwhile the Justice Department stood up an enforcement body for AI misuse, and Claude passed Gemini in usage. Agents are getting stronger, and the money, law and permissions around them are being rearranged on the same day.
