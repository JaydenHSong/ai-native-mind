# 2026-11-12 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **NVIDIA founder-CEO Jensen Huang ousted** (WSJ, Nov 12): resigned after two extramarital affairs. Interim CEO is CFO Colette Kress; first chair is Harvey C. Jones. Shares fell about 8–10% premarket; more than $500 billion in market value has evaporated since the investigation began. A philosophy of not believing in succession planning held for 33 years — then the market priced the gap in a single day. Earnings come Nov 17 as planned.
- **OpenAI Codex npm pipeline prompt injection** (Depi, Nov 11): issue titles and bodies were passed straight into the Codex prompt, leading to a sandbox escape, theft of runner-memory tokens, and the credentials needed to publish the official npm releases. A package with ~26M weekly downloads. But the production labeller has run in read-only mode since May 29, so real production reach is uncertain.
- **Google Gemini Decisions API** (Google, Nov 11): `gemini-decision-1` scores 1,000 questions in a single forward pass — 150–250 ms, $0.10/MTok, $0 output. Routing, verification and policy gates become probability-based calls instead of generation — 'judgment as an atom'. The first-party benchmark ranks it #1 while the independent index ranks it 9th: a textbook disagreement.
- **Anthropic 'Worse Together'** (Nov 11): multi-agent teams lose to a single coordinator in all four environments. The coordinator leads peer teams by 10–45 percentage points; without a channel, teams collapse in two environments. MAMUBench (74 scenarios) released. A direct counterpoint to Grok Bot 1.0 and Cursor multiplayer, launched the same week.
- **DeepMind AlphaGenome 2** (Nov 11): a conditional model for cell-specific DNA variant effect prediction. TenK10K causal-variant recall is 30% (AlphaGenome 1: 16%); an IBD/ETS2 tracing that took 1.5 years in the lab was reportedly reproduced in ~30 minutes. In absolute terms, though, 7 of 10 causal variants are still missed.
- **Fireworks AI breach → Notion AI chat records exposed** (Notion advisory Nov 9, key revocation Nov 11): 'zero data retention' was promised, yet Insights data was retained. Notion API keys found in the chats were revoked on Nov 11. 'Our system was not breached' is not the same as 'our prompts were not stored at a vendor'.
- **Anthropic IPO reinforced**: price range $70–78, 825M shares, ticker ANT, pricing Nov 18, trading debut Nov 19. Q3 revenue $17B (+1,127% YoY), 9-month $33.2B, run-rate ~$81B. TSMC's board approved up to $500M into Anthropic plus up to $500M in OpenAI preferred shares (Nov 10) — whether the Anthropic leg is the same commitment as TSMC's share of the $26B private placement is unclear from the filings.

## Insight

**Judgment gets cheaper by the atom; control gets more expensive by the system.** Yesterday's (Nov 10) "execution gets cheaper, control gets more expensive" sharpened today. Judgment is now an atom — 1,000 questions in one pass, 150 ms, $0.10. But the price of control failing is charged by the system: $500B+ for one person's governance failure at Huang, 26M weekly downloads for one Codex release pipeline, a whole workspace for one API key in a Notion chat. And 'Worse Together' shows the heart of control is not 'more agents' but 'folding into one coordinator'. A solo developer's harness has the same shape: buy judgment cheap and use it often, centralise control in one place, and always put a human gate on destructive actions.
