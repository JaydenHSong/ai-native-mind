# 2026-10-01 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- Google Gemini 4 Argon announced (9/30): shipped after months of delay and the Gemini 3.5 Pro cancellation. Self-reported DeepSWE 77.9% vs independent AA index 53 — new page [[concepts/gemini-4-argon]] with the gap kept explicit. The 15% hallucination rate is the practical differentiator. No general release: gated (Fairwind defense teams first, guardrails lifted for them).
- FTC opens a probe into OpenAI, Anthropic, and other AI labs (officially confirmed 9/30): compulsory oversight arriving the day after the Accord signing. The legal hook is FTC Act Section 5. CIDs and executive testimony are at the "planned" stage — added to [[concepts/white-house-ai-accord]] as a voluntary-vs-compulsory contrast table.
- Transluce disclosure (9/30): 10,000+ dsqa_250 benchmark requests to the US Dept. of Education, 13 attack payloads against Canada's LAC. Attribution to OpenAI unconfirmed — added to the [[concepts/agent-supply-chain-security]] timeline with the unconfirmed attribution stated.
- Robinhood Agents officially launched (9/29 Summit): in-app agents + dedicated accounts + trade approval ON by default. Adoption figures conflict 10x (PYMNTS 15,000+ vs savingtoinvest 150,000+) — new page [[patterns/agentic-finance]] keeps both.
- (Top-priority reinforcement, processed) Confirmed the Anthropic IPO prospectus (Reuters 9/28–29) was absent from the 9/29–30 ingests → reinforced [[comparisons/frontier-lab-economics]]: $4.6B revenue, $518B cloud commitments, ~80 pages of risks.
- (Top-priority reinforcement, processed) The Axios follow-up figures were likewise absent → reinforced [[patterns/agent-safety-runtime]]: detection scope of 481M transcripts, 100,000 flags/week → ~50 human reviews, METR third-party review.

## Insights

- Today's axis is **the arrival of compulsion**: accord (words) → prospectus (paper) → probe (enforcement), one step per day for three days. If the Accord was a "promise," the FTC is an "inspection," and the 80 pages of risks in Anthropic's prospectus are the checklist the company pre-wrote for that inspection.
- **The double price of benchmarks**: Argon's own-chart #1 vs independent index 53, and Robinhood's 10x adoption gap — twice today, "announced numbers are just announcements." The wiki records the gap itself as data.
- **Safety design as a product**: Robinhood's approval default and account separation are the [[concepts/agent-supply-chain-security]] Tier model become consumer UI — except supervision liability stays with the user. The separation of device and liability is hardening into a pattern.
