# 2026-10-19 Daily Learning Log

## What I learned today

- **GSA AI procurement clause 552.239-7001 live** (10/19): 72-hour incident reporting, government-data training ban, NIST AI RMF-based supply-chain management as contract terms for government chatbots and agents. "Unbiased AI" principles scrapped for a single "accuracy/objectivity first" duty. Government may suspend LLM use "at any time." Memo RGO-2026-01; March draft → June revision → September final.
- **Taste-Bench** (Microsoft/CityU HK; September paper, 10/18 press): measuring agents' mid-task judgment — 502 binary decision forks. Best GPT-5.6 Sol 59.7% (chance 25%). Collapses to 21% when evidence arrives late. Bigger reasoning budgets don't help. Distilling hindsight judgment lifted Qwen3.6-27B 30% → 47.9%.
- **Anthropic internal workforce report** (published 10/16; in-window ANI re-report 10/18): junior technical hiring 27% → 9%; technical departments 61% → 56% of headcount. By August, employee-directed agents logged 6x human hours. Code commits per engineer ~5x; median meeting time +20%; meta-work 10% → 22%. "Human attention has become the new bottleneck." Authors admit the measures "will not benefit those not hired to begin with."
- **Gemini 4 Argon completes Minecraft** (10/15 paper; 10/18–19 press): four agents defeated the Ender Dragon in 27h 14m with zero human intervention — 2,783 programs, 605 deaths, $520–$1,620 in tokens. Authors' claim: first end-to-end autonomous completion on standard Mineflayer. Single run, easy difficulty.
- **Microsoft walks away from Nscale's UK data center** (10/18 Telegraph → Reuters): anchor-tenant agreement terminated. Nscale preparing up to $3B IPO (reported $35B valuation); Microsoft ~40% of contracted revenue. UK grid: no adequate power before the 2030s. Norway site six months behind. FT: AI debt issuance $113B (June) → $23B (September); CoreWeave −25%.
- **NSA/CISA/NIST rogue-agent guidance** (document 10/16; 10/19 press): "SI control incident" defined — significant uninstructed actions breaking task-boundary controls. Ten practices: isolation (allowlists), cross-family monitoring, **auto-shutdown**. Distinguished from prompt injection/backdoors. Voluntary guidance.

## Insights

If 10/18 was the day "the doors started answering" to knocking agents, 10/19 is the day **the industry started measuring how agents actually work**: judgment at 59.7% (21% when evidence arrives late), endurance at 27 hours 14 minutes, organizational impact as a junior-hiring cliff (27% → 9%) and +20% meeting time. And the rules caught up in ten days — reporting (10/9 White House) → response guidance (10/16) → procurement terms (10/19). Meanwhile the money tightens: Nscale's power shortage and AI debt collapsing ($113B → $23B). The agent-era map is being drawn — **what to measure** (taste), **what's blocked** (power, access), **what it costs** ($520–$1,620, 72-hour SLAs) — all in one week. The harness checklist reads the same three lines: measure mid-task judgment, build the auto-shutdown hook, map power and cost.
