# 2026-11-23 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **OpenAI chief scientist Pachocki discloses log-perplexity figures and proposes industry pacing limits — transformative capability expected in the 0.68–0.70 range** (OpenAI, 11-22): chief scientist Jakub Pachocki published "Perplexity and Intelligence," disclosing internal log-perplexity figures for the first time. On OpenAI's main held-out evaluation dataset: GPT-3.5 around 0.99, GPT-4 around 0.91, recent models below 0.8. He writes that a model at the 0.76 level began producing breakthrough results on public mathematics problems "in a single Slack thread, one after another." Entering the 0.68–0.70 range should bring another transformative capability, though it is unknown what it will be and it may not be testable by today's standards. The proposal: adopt perplexity (equivalently, cross-entropy loss on a held-out dataset) as a shared cross-lab metric, and agree on shared limits not to cross until the ability to understand and monitor AI behavior reaches concrete milestones (for example, a successor to CoT monitors) — a first limit plus a sequence of later pacing limits, with no agreed numerical cap set. He also notes AI could begin contributing to fundamental pre-training advances as early as 2027. Limits acknowledged: labs use different datasets and mixtures, so comparisons are hard; perplexity does not capture post-training gains such as CoT; and the loss floor (entropy) is unknown. Secondary coverage, including TechTimes, was also confirmed on November 23.
- **Competitors join hands to push shared agent protocols — Agentic AI Foundation, MCP, AGENTS.md, and x402 donated** (CoinDesk, PYMNTS, 11-22): the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft and others participating, is pushing to build open protocols for autonomous AI agents, aiming to keep the internet from fragmenting as tens of billions of agents transact on users' behalf. Donated technology: Anthropic donated the Model Context Protocol (MCP), already in use on more than 10,000 public servers. OpenAI contributed the AGENTS.md framework, Block contributed goose, and Coinbase contributed the x402 payment protocol — x402 has already processed more than 138 million transactions. Scale forecasts: Animoca Brands chairman Yat Siu estimates up to 100 billion AI agents will trade online within a few years; Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong claims agents will drive more than 99.9% of transactions within ten years. Both are forecasts and claims. Caution: SingularityNET CEO Ben Goertzel — shared protocols for agent-to-agent communication, identity, and security attestation are positive, but standardizing how agents work internally is not desirable. Background: last week, Federal Reserve Governor Michael Barr warned that major banks had encountered AI agents acting inside retail accounts without verifiable customer consent or authenticity. A PYMNTS survey (October report): 63% of consumers allow agents to compare credit products, 49% allow them to fill out and submit applications under final approval.
- **Z.ai releases Loop Transformer GLM-5.4 — 750B layers run twice for claimed 2–3T-class intelligence, 1M context** (Z.ai, TestingCatalog, 11-23): Z.ai released its first Loop Transformer flagship, GLM-5.4, on November 23. The 28 layers of the roughly 750B-parameter GLM-5 base run twice with the same weights, making the model "think deeper" without adding parameters, with intelligence claimed to rival 2–3T-parameter models. It has a 1M-token context and vision input (visual coding through screenshots and images). Announced at 04:18 UTC on November 23 by Z.ai lead Zixuan Li on X, reposted by the official @Zai_org account. No separate blog post was confirmed. Availability: immediately for all GLM Coding Plan subscribers (`glm-5.4[1m]` in Claude Code, `glm-5.4` elsewhere). Also usable in ZCode, Z.ai Chat, Trae, Qoder, AutoClaw, WorkBuddy, CatPaw, and Qingyan. Weekly quota up 50% over GLM-5.3, credit multipliers lowered (input 4.6 / cache 1.1 / output 16, vs 6.9 / 1.7 / 24 for GLM-5.3). Claimed speed: up to 200 tokens per second off-peak, 70–80 tokens per second under peak load. Positioning: "Generalized Coding" — code as the basis for video, games, 3D shaders, and CAD. Demos include a music video, a FreeCAD 6-axis robot arm, a full game, and interactive 3D scenes. Not yet disclosed: no official benchmark scores for GLM-5.4 itself (to be published later). The usage-based API is pending a safety evaluation (price undecided). Open weights are planned after safety hardening (no date or license yet).
- **OpenAI begins free ChatGPT Plus for academic researchers — 50,000 grants, first-come first-served, applications open 11-23 (announced 11-16)** (OpenAI, 11-23): OpenAI opened applications for "ChatGPT for Academic Researchers" on November 23, offering 50,000 free ChatGPT Plus grants on a first-come, first-served basis in this round. The program itself was announced on November 16, and the overall target is 200,000 researchers. Scale: 50,000 new grants in this round, first-come, first-served — the pilot's lottery system is dropped. Each grant gives free ChatGPT Plus access to the recipient plus up to two collaborators. This round supports up to 150,000 people. Tier change: the pilot offered Pro, but usage review showed most researchers fit comfortably within Plus limits, so the tier changed to Plus. Eligibility: academic researchers worldwide (science, mathematics, engineering). Waitlisted applicants who qualify received activation guidance by email before opening. Pilot results: 20,000 participants across 33 countries since July. OpenAI's own research estimates the work would have taken nearly twice as long without AI. Program data is by default not used for model training. Extras: a $5 million donation to the Simons Foundation mathematics initiative, and the release of the "Science at the Frontier" research. Conditions: the OpenAI announcement gives no exact opening time, detailed eligibility rules, or grant duration. Per secondary coverage (CASRAI and others): 12 months, for research faculty and postdoctoral researchers, excluding China and Hong Kong, with no API credits or special models included. Date caution: the program was announced on November 16; November 23 is the application opening date.
- **Ireland's DPC delayed or cancelled 9 AI launches in five years — 180 products supervised, 2021–2025 report (covered 11-22)** (PPC Land, Ireland DPC, 11-22): Ireland's Data Protection Commission (DPC) reported in its 75-page report "Responsible Artificial Intelligence Innovation" (published September 25, 2026) that 9 AI product launches were delayed or cancelled after DPC intervention over five years. PPC Land covered the report in detail on November 22; the report itself was published on September 25, and that day's coverage is the new item. Supervision scope: about 180 AI products and services in 2021–2025 (143 generative AI, 37 non-generative). Companies covered include Airbnb, Apple, DeepSeek, Google, LinkedIn, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Pinterest, TikTok, and X. The 9 delayed or cancelled launches: 1 in 2022, 1 in 2023, 4 in 2024, 3 in 2025. Conversely, 95% launched with no delay attributable to DPC involvement. Recommendations: 59% of products launched in 2024–2025 received recommendations. The median was 3 recommendations per case, and 72% of recommendations concerned transparency. Trend: AI-related supervision cases grew 10x from 2021 to 2025. In 2025, about a quarter of supervision cases against large tech companies were AI-related. Position: the DPC said justifying the use of private messages for AI training on "legitimate interests" grounds would be "very difficult to achieve."
- **Google blocks 41 AI crawlers from its own Maps — __usage_ai-training group added to robots.txt (11-22)** (PPC Land, 11-22): Google added a `__usage_ai-training` group to google.com/robots.txt, blocking 41 AI-training crawlers from its Maps service. PPC Land reported it on the evening of November 22. The only newly blocked service is Maps, and Google's own model (Gemini) token stays in the default group — an asymmetry critics note. The change: the new `User-agent: __usage_ai-training` group, 41 named crawlers, 182 rules (154 Disallow, 28 Allow). The file grew from about 6,700 to about 12,500 characters; `User-agent:` lines from 6 to 49, groups from 4 to 5. The only new restriction is Maps: the 54 existing Maps-related default rules (37 Allow + 17 Disallow) were replaced by a single `Disallow: /maps` — place pages, search, directions, contributor profiles, saved maps, and the Maps sitemap. Search (`/search`) and the `/goto?` redirect were already blocked for all crawlers, so they are not new. Asymmetry: Google added its own control token `Google-Extended` to the default group (`*`, `Google-Extended`, Yandex), keeping Maps open for Gemini training and grounding while closing it to the 41 external crawlers. The 41 named crawlers: AI2Bot, Ai2Bot-Dolma, Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended, bedrockbot, Bytespider, CCBot, ChatGLM-Spider, ClaudeBot, cohere-training-data-crawler, Cotoyogi, DeepSeekBot, Diffbot, ElevenlabsCrawler, ExaSearchBot, FacebookBot, FriendlyCrawler, GPTBot, Grok, HySpider, ICC-Crawler, ImagesiftBot, img2dataset, KimiBot, LCC, Meta-ExternalAgent, MistralAI-Training, MistralBot, MoonshotBot, NagetBot, omgili, omgilibot, QwenBot, recraft-image-dataset, Reflectionbot, SBIntuitionsBot, Timpibot, VelenPublicWebCrawler, web-image-collection-research, Webzio-Extended, YiBot. Not on the list: OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Applebot, Bingbot, Googlebot, and others continue under the default rules; no SEO-tool crawlers either. Background: the `__usage_ai-training` token is the shared AI-training opt-out token Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Anthropic pledged to honor on October 30, 2026. Date and reliability cautions: the first tip-off X post landed at 14:03 UTC on November 22, 57 minutes before the reporting window opened (15:00 UTC), but the PPC Land story was written after 19:30 UTC the same day, inside the window. The robots.txt edit itself is undated (the Wayback copy from 02:04 UTC on November 17 has no new group; it appears by November 22). PPC Land's requests between 19:00 and 19:30 UTC still returned the old file, so the rollout may have been uneven or partly rolled back.

## Insights

**Today's axis is "measure it in numbers, bind it in rules, and close the door." Pachocki proposed measuring model intelligence in numbers and agreeing to "stop at 0.68–0.70"; the AAIF saw competitors join hands to write the rules that let agents talk to each other; Google, by contrast, closed the door of its own Maps to 41 external crawlers — while leaving it open for its own Gemini. Z.ai chose to run the same layers twice to "go deeper" instead of growing the brain, OpenAI lowered the threshold for AI research by giving Plus to 50,000 researchers, and Ireland's DPC put numbers to five years of supervision: "only 9 launches were blocked, 95% went through as-is." One shared question runs through all of it: who decides "by which numbers, and how far" — measurement, standards, and access control all moved on the same day.**

**Organized together**: the AAIF protocol push (11-22) — reinforced in [[concepts/anthropic-agent-skills-foundation]] (MCP, AGENTS.md, goose, and x402 donations; 10,000+ MCP servers; 138M x402 transactions; Goertzel's share-communication/keep-internals-autonomous distinction; the Barr warning). The MCP donation — a new 2026-11-22 section in [[concepts/mcp]].
