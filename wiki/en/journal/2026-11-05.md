# 2026-11-05 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **Cat Wu joins OpenAI**: Claude Code product lead Catherine Wu announced on 2026-11-05 that she is joining OpenAI to lead product management for Core Products, after her last day at Anthropic on 2026-10-30. Two Claude Code engineers also left the same week. A core talent departure just before Anthropic's IPO.
- **Apple's 10-K on agent risk**: Apple named autonomous AI agents' unauthorized actions, access to systems without permission, and hard-to-reverse outcomes as a business risk in its Form 10-K filed 2026-11-03. Its AI risk disclosure grew from 199 words to 628 words.
- **Kimi K3.1 Pro weights released**: Moonshot published the full 2.8T MoE weights (1.56 TB) on Hugging Face on the promised date, 2026-11-04. The license requires a separate agreement for model-as-a-service businesses with more than $20M revenue over 12 months.
- **ChatGPT Images 2.6**: OpenAI released Images 2.6 on 2026-11-04 with animated GIF creation, SVG export and saved reference images. The API gains two models, Flare and Sunburst; token rates are unchanged, but low-quality images now use about 4.2x the output tokens. About 4 billion images are generated per week.
- **Tencent Hy4 full release**: Hy4 launched on 2026-11-04 as the first Hunyuan model with vision and computer use. It scores 78.9 on OSWorld 2.1, close to Claude's 81.8, but the weights are still pending, promised "in the coming days."
- **CJEU's first generative AI case**: Advocate General Szpunar opined on 2026-11-03 that AI training is in principle permitted under the text and data mining exception, while a chatbot's real-time reuse of a press article may infringe the publisher's rights in certain circumstances. The opinion is not binding.

## Insights

The day's thread is that "the boundaries of control were drawn at once — in law, in disclosure, and in talent." Apple put agent misbehavior into an SEC filing, the CJEU drew a legal line between training and answering, and control moves continued after Goldman Sachs the day before. On the same day, Kimi published its weights on schedule while Hy4 opened hosting only and deferred its weights — the test for "open" is now whether the download exists. On talent, Claude Code's product know-how moving to OpenAI confirms the agent race is being fought over people, not just models.
