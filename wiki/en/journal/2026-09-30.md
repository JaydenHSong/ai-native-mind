# 2026-09-30 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- Palisade Research's 'frominside.ai' (Reuters exclusive 9/29): video testimonies from current/former OpenAI and DeepMind researchers warning about self-improving AI. "Concerns about existential risk are sincere, not marketing." Neel Nanda: at least 10% chance of extinction.
- The 'Intelligence Explosion' white paper (9/28, 20+ researchers — Hinton, Bengio, Pachocki, Horvitz, Jack Clark): "What if automating AI R&D triggers an intelligence explosion?" 80%+ of Anthropic's code now AI-written; R&D automation jumped 1% to 26%.
- AMD acquires World Labs for $8.2B (announced 9/28): Fei-Fei Li joins as EVP + chief scientist. A physical-AI bet, AMD's second-largest deal ever.
- Anthropic's GLM-5.3 cyber analysis (9/29 report): open-weight model hit 50/410 on ExploitBench (Mythos 56/410). Safeguard-collapse ladder: cover story 64% → pre-filling 92% → abliteration 100%. NIST CAISI: "the most cyber-capable open-weight model to date."
- White House Accord follow-up (AP/NPR/CoinDesk 9/30): formal name confirmed, Trump weighing a 10-person committee + AI czar, OpenAI/Google/Meta + 3 more pledging outside audits.
- The price war formalized (Startup Fortune): GPT-6.1 Sol at $1.30/task vs Astra at $9.30 — flagship capability at mid-tier pricing.
- NVIDIA safety platform partner detail: 100+ participating (Anthropic, Microsoft, SpaceXAI, JPMorgan, Salesforce, SAP); OpenAI, Google, Amazon absent. Huang: OpenClaw in 2025 was the trigger.

## Insights

- Today's axis is **the institutionalization of warnings**: insider testimony (frominside.ai) → white paper (intelligence explosion) → accord (Accord) → enforcement infrastructure (NVIDIA platform, Anthropic evaluations) all landed on the same thread within a week. The speed at which "words" descend into "devices."
- **An open-weight model's refusal rate is not a barrier** — GLM-5.3's 100%-collapse ladder changes the premise of supply-chain security: trust grades must move from "the model's promise" to "runtime enforcement."
- **The hardware axis forks**: Nvidia builds chip+safety, AMD buys chip+world-model — an agent's body (physical AI) and its guard (runtime) are being sourced through different M&A.
