# 2026-10-25 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **Microsoft Agensh** (10-24): open-source multi-agent harness without a central orchestrator. Scaled 1 → 1,024 agents; ProgramBench mean 19.31% → 28.78% at 128 agents, pandoc 79.59% (GPT-6.1-sol + 32 agents). Coordination moved from prompts to infrastructure.
- **UK AI Biosecurity Lab** (10-24): £100M government cash plus OpenAI £100M and Anthropic up to £100M in credits to found the independent non-profit AIBL. Part of a £165M initiative inside a ~£600M AI security package. Cash versus in-kind is the key distinction.
- **Washington's pressure on AI CEOs** (10-24): Axios reports the White House and Congress telling CEOs to "shut up and focus on product safety." Bipartisan voices (Curtis, Khanna); the trigger was Amodei's safety blog and Anthropic's incident-disclosure timing dispute. Political pressure, not law.
- **Sweden blocks ChatGPT and Claude** (effective 10-26): the Government Offices block both chatbots on staff work PCs, approving only Copilot. The reason is uncontracted use, not model danger. Some staff keep access through an in-house platform.
- **Pennsylvania AI ad disclosure law** (signed 10-22, effective 12-21): SB 806, Act 99 of 2026. Disclosure required only for misleading synthetic ads — a targeted rule, not a blanket label. Senate 48–2, House 163–40.
- **Block Dessa** (unveiled 10-23, covered 10-24): a full-duplex voice AI trained on speech alone, no text pipeline. Single GB200 NVL72 rack, claimed local execution on M4 Max MacBook Pro. Research preview; no parameters or benchmarks published; scam-misuse concerns reported alongside.

## Insights

- **The day of gate design**: all six stories are about gates — who may act, through which path, under what condition. Agensh's claim/merge rules, AIBL's institutional design, Washington's political pressure, Sweden's contract gate, Pennsylvania's disclosure trigger, Dessa's removed text gate and the scam gate it raises.
- **From approval to verified approval**: yesterday's "spreading pre-gates" theme sharpened today — Sweden blocks the uncontracted path, banks block unattributable instructions, and approval criteria are shifting from the model itself to path, contract, and disclosure.
- **Read the number, then read what it is**: £300M of which £100M is cash, a $3,000 fine cap from a single outlet, "hundreds of times fewer words" as a company claim — today's stories all demand checking what a number actually denotes. The wiki's habit of noting source and confirmation status is itself a quality control.
- **Voice as a separate branch**: Dessa is not an extension of text LLMs but a separate architecture branch — voice products gain a design option beyond the text pipeline, and with it, identity verification becomes part of the product spec.
