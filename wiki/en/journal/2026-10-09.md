## Insights

"**Safety is culture, not policy**": the heart of the OpenAI firing dispute is not whether a policy was broken but whether the remaining researchers will speak up — with collaboration norms for outside evaluators like METR still unsettled, the boundary dispute itself determines the quality of safety research. Landing in the same week as the [[concepts/self-improving-ai-risk]] warnings is no coincidence.

"**The week answers became apps**": GPT-6 Intelligent UI (1.2B consumers) and the Gemini agent (enterprise) are two faces of the same turn — AI output moving from "words" to "operable interfaces." The UI layer of [[patterns/agentic-commerce]] got laid this week.

"**Geopolitics raises valuations**": Meta's $2B offer for Manus was blocked by the NDRC, and the lab raised $500M at roughly double that valuation — sanctions and restrictions paradoxically raising the price of independent labs. A second capital-concentration hit on agent labs after Nous ($1.5B, 10/7).

"**Robots get their scaling laws**": RoboJEPA's second-order power law plus the capability-threshold map is robotics' Kaplan/Chinchilla moment. If imagination error becomes a proxy for real-robot evaluation, the cost structure of robotics research changes.
