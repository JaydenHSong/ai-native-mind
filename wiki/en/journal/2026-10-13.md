# 2026-10-13 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **Meta Muse Wonder·Wonder Full** (10/12): the first closed frontier since Llama 4. Agent-specialized with Active Memory and Active Focus, API at $4/$20 (the same list price as Opus 5.5). The shift from the open-Llama era to paid agent infrastructure.
- **Google Workspace MCP + agentic OAuth** (10/12): from 11/11, Workspace MCP calls from OAuth clients without the agentic designation are blocked; the setting locks permanently on 11/12. The first enforced case of platform-level "agent identity attestation" on top of the MCP standard.
- **Meta×Sierra Poppy draft 0.1** (10/9): the personal agent protocol. View-only vs change, business-set limits — the third option between "block" and "full delegation." 35 design partners (including OpenAI, Visa, Stripe).
- **DigitalOcean Agent Learning Services** (10/12): productization of the Insights→Signals→Evaluations & Simulations loop. The signal that agent observability is becoming general-purpose cloud infrastructure.
- **Brad Smith's five mandatory rules** (10/12): "AI is less regulated than washing machines and milk." Global safety rules, victim notification, kill switch, mandatory incident reporting, pre-release independent evaluation. Two days after Nadella.
- **HCLTech×Anthropic** (10/12): the Global Premier Partnership. Anthropic Business Unit + FDE dispatch. Enterprise agentic AI moves from experiments to governance-embedded deployment at scale.

## Insights

Today's news converges into one line: **"who an agent is" is becoming infrastructure.** Google enforces the agentic identity in OAuth, Meta×Sierra ship the commerce identity standard, and Anthropic sells the governance identity through the SI channel. If model competition (yesterday's Maia 300 and Wonder) was the "architecture of money," today the "architecture of identity" is layered on top. For the harness designer this is practical: separate the agentic client ID before 11/11, and design audit logs as compliance assets.
