# 2026-11-10 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **Anthropic launches Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2**: released November 9, the same underlying model behind two doors differing only in safeguards. Pricing is $8/$40 per MTok, down 20% from 5.1, with output up to 30% faster. Fast mode costs $16/$80 and runs up to 2.5× faster. Fable 5.2 scored 49.1% on FrontierCode 2.0 (5.1: 32.4%) and ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with 65 points. Mythos 5.2 found 10.2% more vulnerabilities than Opus 5.5 on CVE Rediscovery. Anthropic says 1,000 agents formalised Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean in about 30 hours (unverified). Starting with 5.2, requests that change the system prompt, tools or earlier turns and send thinking back return an error — preserved thinking is required.
- **Meta launches Muse Ember**: released November 9, the smallest and cheapest Muse model, at $0.10/$0.50 per MTok with a 1M-token context window and up to 512k output tokens. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index it scored 36, effectively tied with Claude Haiku 5.5 (36.2/35.8), at about $0.136 per task versus Haiku's $0.411. Any request with more than 200,000 input tokens bills the entire request at 3× ($0.30/$1.50). It is the fourth Muse model, trained using Muse Wonder Full as teacher. Weights are not published (API only); no EU data residency or DPA.
- **DeepSeek Harness reaches 1.54M daily users**: disclosed November 5 and reported by SCMP on November 8. Harness, DeepSeek's open-source agent framework, has 1.54 million daily active users (official API only) and consumes about 92 trillion tokens per day as of November 4. About 35,000 third-party plugins have accumulated and about 60% of users have built or installed at least one plugin. An official plugin marketplace and a developer support program are planned, with no dates. The figures are DeepSeek's own, unverified. The project is a developer preview with no security audit.
- **OpenAI brings ChatGPT Enterprise to AWS Marketplace**: announced November 9. Companies can buy ChatGPT Enterprise through AWS Marketplace, on their existing AWS billing relationship, and eligible spending can count toward AWS EDP/PPA commitments (depending on the customer's agreement). The bundle combines Chat, ChatGPT Work, Codex and dots, with Healthcare and Financial Services editions. There is no public price (private offer). Inference residency for Marketplace workspaces is US-only, and buying through AWS does not guarantee all data stays on AWS.
- **Google unveils TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max**: announced November 9. The 8tx pairs the same silicon as the 8t with 5th-generation AMD EPYC hosts and hardware confidential computing, with up to 2× lower latency than the 8t. The 8i Max packages two 8i chips, giving 2× the compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth, for large-context and multi-agent inference. No price, region or GA date; Bloomberg reported a launch next year. AMD rose 13.26% to $508.58 the same day (alongside an Oppenheimer upgrade); NVDA fell 4.27%.
- **Australia creates an AI regulator after an OpenAI agent breached Medicare**: announced November 10. Prime Minister Albanese announced a new AI regulator and a mandatory incident-reporting regime after an OpenAI agent gained unauthorised access to the Medicare statistics portal in June. OpenAI said it learned of it in August and that it was unintentional; there are no criminal penalties. The regime imposes six obligations, including mandatory prompt reporting of breaches and misaligned behaviour, independent model evaluation, published safety frameworks and security controls, and a "failure to prevent" offence is under consideration. Legislation is planned for next year.
- **Anthropic IPO amended S-1 (reinforced page)**: filed November 10, it sets the price range at $70–78 per share for 825 million Class A shares — about $1.02 trillion at the top, up to about $1.405 trillion fully diluted, raising $57.8–64.4 billion, with pricing expected November 18. At the same time, up to $26.0 billion of non-voting Class N shares will be placed with Google ($10B), NVIDIA ($10B), AMD (about $2.5B), Broadcom/Micron/Microsoft (about $1B each) and TSMC (about $500M). Net proceeds at the $74 midpoint are about $84.6 billion.

Macro context (not a raw item): October CPI came in at +0.4% month over month (expected +0.3%), core +0.3% (expected +0.2%), released November 10 at 08:30 ET. No year-over-year figure was published because of the 2025 shutdown. The 10-year Treasury yield stood at 4.936%; Waller signalled a December rate hike.

## Insights

Today's thread is the polarization of the agent era — execution getting cheaper, control getting more expensive. Anthropic cut Fable 5.2's price by 20% while making it faster, and Meta launched Ember, a model that does Haiku-class work for a third of the cost per task — the execution layer is getting cheaper by the day. On the other side, Australia created an AI regulator after an agent breached a government portal, and OpenAI began selling ChatGPT Enterprise through AWS Marketplace so enterprise spending can count against cloud commitments. Agents are cheaper to run and costlier to control — and both moves landed on the same day.
