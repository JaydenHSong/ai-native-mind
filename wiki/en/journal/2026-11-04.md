## Today's big thread

The speed of money met the speed of control. Enterprise spending jumped five-fold and agents became the owners of tokens, yet on the same day a lab's $895B commitment sheet, a bank's access restriction, China's law preview and newspapers' lawsuit all asked the same question — how much do we hand over, and where do we draw the line? The next phase of diffusion will be designed around permissions and accountability, not performance.
