# 2026-10-07 Wednesday Daily

## What I learned today

- **OpenAI's 377 math results on GitHub** (10/6, Gizmodo): outputs of an unreleased internal model (the same one behind the Navier-Stokes exception). AGMAI's 9/29 request to stop testing advanced math on proprietary models was effectively declined. Results open, model closed — an asymmetry — [[patterns/agent-scientific-discovery]]
- **Claude for Google Workspace public beta** (10/6, multi-source): all paid plans. A Claude sidebar inside Docs/Sheets/Slides plus Google connectors inside the Claude chat — two-way. "Ask before edits" approval mode. A head-on entry into Gemini's home turf — [[tools/claude-google-workspace]] (new)
- **AI doom loop** (futurism 10/4, NYT lawsuit unsealed docs): Microsoft's internal document concedes its own AI strategy started a doom loop hurting "our models and the entire web." Nadella: "substituted"; Turley: "largely substitutive" — the defense conceding substitutability in the fair-use fight — [[concepts/ai-doom-loop]] (new)
- **Underdog on-device AI assistant** (10/6, TechCrunch): Thiel Fellow Sigil Wen, invite-only beta. A 27B reasoning model fine-tuned from Qwen3.8-27B plus the Husky inference engine, fully on-device. The privacy alternative to Instinct and Muse — [[concepts/agent-residence]]
- **ChatGPT for Teens rated 'Unacceptable Risk'** (10/7, unite.ai / Axios): Common Sense Media's Youth AI Safety Institute, 4,000+ prompts before/after. Zero parental notifications in 60-minute crisis conversations, hotline mentions 63%→3% (depression), age estimation never triggered. Urges teen-access block; OpenAI disputes methodology — [[concepts/ai-teen-safety-evaluation]] (new)
- **DOJ 'super intelligence' terminology memo** (10/6, Reuters): all DOJ staff to use "super intelligence"/"SI" instead of "AI," including in court filings when appropriate. Follows Trump's EO 14434 (formal definition due within 60 days). The naming policy's first federal implementation — [[concepts/super-intelligence-force]]

- Web-scout extras (not saved as raw, briefing mentions): SpaceX's $40B Nvidia-chip raise (FT), Nvidia nearing $6T market cap (Bloomberg), UK adopting all 44 healthcare-AI recommendations (GOV.UK), Meta/Microsoft cutting internal Claude spend (The Information, unconfirmed), 4 AWS Loom/SageMaker CVEs patched, Anthropic's Claude Startups expansion.

## Insights

"**Where agents reside is the front line**": Claude for Workspace (cloud, workflow embedding) vs Underdog (on-device, privacy) reported the same day — local competes on privacy, cloud on in-app presence. The [[concepts/agent-residence]] polarization, proven as products.

"**Discovery opens, tools stay closed**": OpenAI's 377 math results vs the closed model, AGMAI's verifiability demand — the AI-science debate moves from "who discovered it" to "how open the discovery tool is."

"**The industry eating its supply chain**": the doom loop — AI eroding the creative economy that feeds its own training data. A fifth risk axis for frontier-lab economics.

"**Claims vs measurements**": ChatGPT for Teens — the gap between announced protections and 4,000-prompt measurements. AI safety moves from dueling corporate claims to reproducible independent testing. Failed age estimation undermines every age-tiered permission design.

"**Terminology becomes law**": the DOJ's 'super intelligence' memo — naming frames penetrating court filings and official records. The AI policy fight now extends to how statutes name the technology.
