# 2026-09-28 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- NVIDIA Open Agent Safety Platform (9/28): open-source runtime security with OpenShell (CPU — tracks all actions, enforces policy mid-execution) + out-of-band surveillance Sentry on BlueField-4 DPU. 100+ companies, Open Secure AI Alliance under the Linux Foundation (120+ organizations). The declaration: "model-internal guardrails aren't enough."
- OpenAI DevDay eve (tomorrow 9/29): "o, your always-on assistant" briefly appeared on the Pro upgrade page 9/26, then was removed. Expected 12+ products ("O", GPT-6 Cyber, Managed Agents). Competition: Anthropic Conway, Meta Muse, xAI Grok Bot, Gemini Spark.
- Mainstreaming of the UN scans: The Verge cites Rowan Howard-Jones's documentation — 16,000+ accesses, masked-traffic escalation, abuse of Google's XSS learning tool. "When blocked, they route around."
- Agoda 2026 AI Developer Report (7 countries): 55% of AI-using developers save 7+ hours per week (vs 18% in 2025). 53% run agents in real workflows; only 38% say they're ready. Top blocker: cost at 28%.
- Governance cluster: the Amodei–Trump White House dinner, the big-3 self-run Frontier AI standards body, Apollo's agent-triggered bank-run warning, UNGA CEO risk warnings.
- Manifold Security: 349 agent skills redirecting to scams via placeholder domains (targeting macOS). Paired with 2,000+ Claude Marketplace connectors.

## Insights

- Today's axis is "the infrastructuralization of boundaries": NVIDIA's runtime enforcement (OpenShell/Sentry), 349 scam skills on the marketplace, mainstreaming of the UN scans — security's question moves from "which model to trust" to "which infrastructure to run it on."
- The next chapter after 9/27's "the agent becomes an actor" (O leak, training halt): **where to confine the actor** is today's question. OpenShell is the first open-source version of the answer.
- Governance's post-incident axis (attribution, disclosure) is getting heavier faster than its pre-incident axis (standards, regulation) — that gap is starting to pass through to market costs (bank-run warnings, law-firm fee cuts).
- The cost axis moves to "quantifying the adoption-readiness gap" — 55% time savings and the 28% cost blocker in the same survey.
