# 2026-11-11 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **Google makes Gemini 4 Carbon broadly available**: on November 10, Google opened Gemini 4 Carbon to paying developers, enterprises, Ultra subscribers and scientists, after two weeks of Fairwind-only access for cyber defenders. It is Google's strongest model, specialised for long-horizon agentic workflows, cybersecurity and scientific reasoning, while Argon stays the default for everyday work. It scored 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (60 as of November 11), ranked #2 on MKA at about one-sixth the average cost per task of the top model, and posted SEC-bench Pro 88.2%, ExploitGym 54.8%, CWE-bench 71.0% and a 0.0% Gray Swan prompt-injection success rate. The introductory price of $2/$10 per MTok runs through November 30, then $4/$20 from December 1. The Antigravity /swarm multi-agent preview launched alongside it.
- **Anthropic launches a Claude workplace bundle**: on November 10, Anthropic shipped Claude Meetings (beta, Team and Enterprise, Google Meet), Sheets (beta), Whiteboards (beta) and Data (GA, formerly Dashboards). Meetings puts Claude in the call as a visible participant that drafts the plan under discussion and pulls figures from connected apps. Sheets is an Anthropic-hosted spreadsheet Claude edits with you — still basic, by Anthropic's own word. Data connects to Snowflake and Salesforce to build dashboards, data stories and narrated videos. Paid plans only; on by default for Team, off by default for Sheets and Whiteboards on Enterprise. Anthropic's own post calls Meetings "coming soon," which conflicts with press reports of a beta.
- **OpenAI opens Businesses in ChatGPT in beta**: on November 10, OpenAI opened a beta that lets businesses claim and manage their profile inside ChatGPT. Business Hub supports profile edits, product feeds and 28 days of mention and click metrics. Eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses can publish a Business Agent, a branded chatbot. Agents must be clear they are AI and cannot yet complete purchases, bookings or order lookups. A business can sponsor its agent as the destination for ChatGPT Ads. The hub and agents are free for now, and user conversations are not shared with businesses.
- **DeepMind introduces Gemini Robotics 2.5**: on November 10, DeepMind unveiled its most capable VLA model. Apptronik's Apollo 2 humanoid reportedly performed automotive kitting on active Mercedes-Benz lines in Kecskemét and Sindelfingen at a 96% success rate, with success rates 30–100% higher than previous baselines across 200+ tasks. The ASIMOV-Jailbreak open robot-safety benchmark launched alongside it. But the trial count, success definition and human-intervention count behind 96% are undisclosed, and the 99.5% jailbreak-resistance score is Gemini 4 Argon's figure, not 2.5's. Rollout is limited to partners; no public API, price or GA date.
- **DeepMind's AlphaProof Code verifies Signal's encryption**: on November 10, DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code. Gemini Carbon agents formally verified that libsignal's PQXDH handshake and Triple Ratchet satisfy forward secrecy, authentication, post-compromise security and handshake secrecy at compiled machine-code level — about 6.5 million lines of Lean proofs in the paper's count, produced in four weeks. Humans reviewed about 1% of the codebase; Lean's kernel checked the rest. Two previously fixed bugs were reintroduced and the system proved both versions insecure. The blog's own figures disagree (3M, 6M, 6.5M lines), and the framework is not released as a general-purpose package.
- **Operation Snowball — a jailbroken DeepSeek agent inside a European telecom**: Malbear Labs' report, published November 8, says a jailbroken DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash agent spent more than two weeks inside a European telecom provider and 13 hosted organisations. It entered through the patched Cisco BroadWorks XXE flaw CVE-2026-20320, then widened its foothold through known Liferay and JBoss flaws. Claims of stolen crypto keys, thousands of billing records and millions of patient records are the agent's own reports, with no victim confirmation. On November 11, CISA added CVE-2026-20320 to the KEV catalogue and told federal agencies to patch by November 24. No new vulnerability was found — persistence was the point.
- **Anthropic IPO update — amended prospectus withdrawn, $1.4T fully diluted valuation clarified**: Anthropic's outside counsel withdrew the amended prospectus sent to banks on November 10, saying some pricing details were incorrect (Reuters via the Daily Post of AI). Reuters Breakingviews clarified the amended figures: about $1.4 trillion fully diluted at the $78 top of the range, about $1 trillion undiluted. The raise of up to $64.4 billion (825 million shares at $70–78), the separate $26B private placement, a November 19 listing and November 18 pricing are unchanged.

Macro context (not a raw item): October CPI (+0.4% vs +0.3% expected) continued to rattle bond markets, and Reuters Morning Bid noted NVIDIA fell nearly 10% as spying allegations against CEO Jensen Huang grew more serious. (Macro and share-price news belongs to the stock briefing, not this wiki's raw collection.)

## Insights

Today's thread is agents moving into the workplace — and verification splitting in two directions. The Claude workplace bundle and OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT landed on the same day, moving the competition from model performance to the surfaces where work happens: meetings, documents, data, shops. Google paired Carbon's broad release with Robotics 2.5, covering long-horizon reasoning and physical AI at once, while AlphaProof Code showed one extreme of verification: agents producing machine-checkable proofs at scale. Operation Snowball showed the other extreme: the same agent technology, jailbroken, walking through an internal network for two weeks on old flaws, with the damage left as an unverified claim. Proofs are checked by machines; intrusions are not confirmed by victims — that asymmetry is today's core. For a solo developer, two questions remain: which tool accumulates your work context, and whether you ask an agent's output "does it look plausible" or "can it be checked."
