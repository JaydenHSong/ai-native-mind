# 2026-10-15 Learning Log

## What I learned today

- **Eight state AGs sue OpenAI** (10/14, azcentral original, Engadget, Bloomberg Law): Arizona AG Kris Mayes leads; Arkansas, Idaho, Montana, Iowa, Kansas, West Virginia, and Nebraska file separate complaints in their own state courts (no joint action). Allegations: ChatGPT designed to maximize compulsive use by minors — "manipulative design tactics," sycophantic alignment. Arizona complaint is 87 pages; demands up to $10,000 per willful violation, plus disgorgement and injunctions against safety claims in other states. OpenAI spokesperson Drew Pusateri disputes the facts. A separate 10/13 class action covers collecting children's data without parental consent. Background: the June Florida AG suit, June multi-state subpoenas, ~51 OpenAI-related cases pending per the AI Lawsuit Tracker. → [[concepts/eight-states-openai-child-safety-lawsuit]]
- **Booking Vayo** (10/14, GeekWire, Skift, Morningstar, PhocusWire): Booking Holdings unveils a Seattle-based AI travel startup. Conditional up-to-$100M investment tied to product progress and market conditions. A personal AI travel agent — "built for trips, not tasks." Connects to the inbox to consolidate booking-confirmation emails into an itinerary, then monitors price drops, sold-out hotel openings, and travel disruptions. Multiple agents running concurrently with per-task model selection; CTO Allen Chen (custom models possible). Restaurant booking works now; flights and hotels within weeks. A neutral orchestrator covering competitors (Expedia, direct bookings) too, with OpenTable/SeatGeek direct partnerships instead of scraping. Booking's second AI-native startup (the first was Lola, two weeks earlier). → [[concepts/booking-vayo-travel-agent]]
- **Apple Intelligence's China launch** (10/14, 9to5Mac, Bloomberg, SCMP): coming to mainland China in December via iOS 27.3, ending a 2-year regulatory delay. Restricted build — the redesigned Siri AI excluded, image-generation tools excluded, text generation limited in some apps, Visual Intelligence excluded. Included: the 2024 original set (Genmoji, notification summaries, writing tools, Clean Up). Concessions: Alibaba co-developed guardrails including a government-approved filter on AI model updates; cloud queries routed through Alibaba (Tongyi Qianwen replacing OpenAI). MIIT network-access licensing on 9/29 (retroactive). China is 17–20% of Apple's revenue; rivals already ship AI features. → [[concepts/apple-intelligence-china-launch]]
- **GPT-4 series bulk retirement** (10/14, official OpenAI email "Plan your transition from GPT-4 series to newer OpenAI models," noreply@email.openai.com → jsong@spigen.com): GPT-4o, 4o mini, 4.1, 4.1 mini retired in one announcement. Claimed reason: stronger capability and safety in newer models. No dates or grace periods named. No press follow-up confirmed. → [[concepts/openai-gpt4-series-retirement]]
- **Nvidia's $250M UK compute pledge** (10/14, Reuters wire): ~$50M/year over five years of compute access via UK cloud partners. Beneficiaries: Oxford BOLD, UCL SOFAIR, Imperial. Research scope: agentic foundation models, safety engineering, robot safety, security agents, open-model security, real-world threat mitigation — using OpenShell/Sentry. Huang: "AI capability and safety must advance together"; UK AI minister Kanishka Narayan. Providers, architecture, and commercial terms undecided. Context: ~$5.8T market cap, the IMF's AI-funding bubble warning, Huang's 10/13–14 Europe tour. → [[concepts/nvidia-uk-ai-safety-compute]]
- **Grok Voice 3.0** (10/14, xAI announcement via Unite.AI, Artificial Analysis): real-time speech-to-speech on the Voice Agent API, available immediately. Price frozen at $0.08/min ($4.80/hour). Artificial Analysis Speech-to-Speech Index at 86.9, #1 (GPT-Live-1 Astra Medium 83.2, Gemini 3.8 Live 82.6). xAI claims: Big Bench Audio 98.5%, Agent Arena Elo 1070, task success 94.8%, Full Duplex Bench 97.0%, $0.025/task (vs $0.030 GPT-Live-1). Deployment claims (unverified): millions of daily conversations in Tesla/SpaceX vehicles, ~70% of Starlink's 20,000 daily calls resolved without humans. → [[concepts/grok-voice-3]]

## Insights

- **The evaluate→demand→sue chain completes**: CSM's 'Unacceptable Risk' verdict (10/7) → the public block-demand (10/14) → eight state AG suits (10/15). An independent evaluation's numbers becoming legal demands within a week means the "civilian evaluation axis" of regulation is now actually moving. Next watch: whether courts accept an "order changing how it operates."
- **Compute diplomacy**: the $250M pledge is about preempting standards, not raising capital — seeding OpenShell/Sentry into national research infrastructure. A new axis after 10/13's "defend with deployment scale" (Maia multi-GW): "preempt with standards."
- **Turnover and premium as a pair**: the GPT-4 retirement notice and GPT-6.1 Ultrafast's 6x premium are the same coin — the old generation must disappear for the premium to persuade. The era when lifecycle notices arrive by email.
- **Residence becomes the sovereignty question**: the China build proves "local ≠ sovereignty" — on-device models still get a government-mandated Alibaba cloud residence, with a model-update approval filter. The residence axes gain "who licenses the residence?"
