## Today's big thread

The control lines were redrawn today. A watermark disappears with one setting change, a security researcher's session becomes training data through one policy change, and malware uses an LLM as an appraiser to rank victims by what they are worth. Two safety mechanisms — marking and consent — wobbled at once. On the other side, agents gained a body (Meta Max), local judgment (Drex Max), and pre-execution verification (PTV). The signal of the day is the widening gap between how fast agents grow capable and how trustworthy the devices controlling them are.
