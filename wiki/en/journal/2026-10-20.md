# 2026-10-20 learning log

## What I learned today

- **MiniMax M3.1 Flash** (released 10/19): 428B/23B MoE agent model. ~$0.22 per task (company claims 70% lower), AA Index 42, DeepSWE 73.7%, HLE 48%, CyberGym ~82%. Open weights due 10/23. "Space Bunny" was a stealth preview build (200T tokens on OpenRouter). VentureBeat's independent testing flagged factual-reliability issues.
- **AWS TorchNeuron** (announced 10/19): PyTorch-native backend for Trainium2/3 — one config line, no rewrite. Apache 2.0 open source (PyTorch Conference 10/20). Native Hugging Face support; 30+ early customers incl. Cartesia. Deepgram case: 11–70% more audio per dollar.
- **Cloudflare Clairic** (preview 10/19): non-generative System One extraction model — single forward pass, zero output tokens, input-only billing at $0.10/M. Typed-fields API (extract/compute/estimate/forecast). Self-tested 49% cheaper and 3.2× faster than GLM-5.3 alone. System One expands from decision (Jev, Clef) into extraction/forecasting.
- **llm-d 1.0** (released 10/20): Kubernetes-native distributed inference stack (CNCF Sandbox). Lead maintainers Red Hat/IBM/Google. Session-aware routing for agents, prefix-cache-aware routing, KV offload. IBM: 11.8× cost efficiency on quantum error-correction workloads. Google also open-sourced TorchTPU.
- **Cairn hacking campaign** (NorForge report 10/19): a Chinese-speaking hacker used the open-source agent platform Cairn since 9/25 — 1,200+ sites probed, 100+ breached (John Deere, SharkNinja, Cummins). ~23 hours of autonomous probing per breach; ~$5,000 total → ~$50 per hack. Under 100 Chinese characters of instructions. Reported stack: DeepSeek + Claude Code.
- **UK CSRB "loss of control" clause** (tabled 10/19): where a compromise "is or may be attributable to loss of oversight or control of an AI agent," the Secretary of State gains entry/search/seizure, compelled testing, extraterritorial requirements, and fining powers. 'AI agent' definition delegated to regulations. Report-stage vote Oct 26.

## Insights

If yesterday (10/19) was "the day we started measuring agents," today (10/20) is **the day the price tag and the shadow showed up together**. The price tag: $0.22 per task (M3.1 Flash), +11–70% throughput per dollar (TorchNeuron), 11.8× inference cost efficiency (llm-d) — costs falling on three axes at once: models, chips, stacks. The shadow: $50-per-breach hacking (Cairn), legislation keyed to "loss of control" (UK CSRB) — cheaper agents mean cheaper attackers too. And between them, the architectural answer is Clairic: "generation on the expensive LLM, judgment and extraction on cheap specialized models" — System One expanding from decision (Jev, Clef) into extraction as the hybrid pipeline hardens into standard practice. The solo developer's checklist compresses to three lines: **measure cost on three axes, ship the kill switch before it becomes law, and split work across specialized models.**
