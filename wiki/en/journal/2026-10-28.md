# 2026-10-28 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **Google Genie 4** (10-27): the first real-time world model with native audio — music, ambient sound, and dialogue generated in the same pass as the visuals. Gemini Omni anchors realism, Gemini 3.8 Flash guides in the loop. Natural-language steering mid-simulation, coherent scenes of about two minutes (double Genie 3). Trusted-tester research preview, then AI Ultra's Project Genie and Gemini API / AI Studio partners. SynthID on all output. Limits: drift, object permanence, legible text.
- **OpenAI's reported Google TPU purchase plan** (10-27 evening): The Information sourcing, terms and scale undisclosed, both companies declined to confirm — confidence medium. TPU veteran hires (Ho and others) and a TPU compiler posting are the clues. A move testing roughly $100B in Nvidia commitments. Follows last week's Google Cloud model deal and chip rentals since June 2025. Amazon, Microsoft, and Alphabet together plan up to $600B in AI infrastructure this year.
- **Anthropic–GSA OneGov expansion** (10-27): 40% discount on token-based usage across all Claude products, no minimum purchase, eligibility extended to federal plus state, local, tribal (and territorial) governments. Direct, reseller, and cloud channels (cloud within months). About 500 organizations already use Claude; weekly actives up about 8x since February, federal civilian usage up about 20x in a year (company figures). The $1-per-user offer expires October 31.
- **Oracle's Vera Rubin expansion and chip diversification** (10-27): OCI expands Vera Rubin to multi-gigawatt scale, value undisclosed (Huang: up to $40B per gigawatt). NVL72 and Vera CPUs generally available, HGX Rubin NVL8 planned. In-house Acceleron networking replaces some Nvidia networking. Google TPUs (8t, 8i) from the first hyperscaler outside Google starting in 2027, plus Cerebras and SambaNova inference chips in 2027. Shares up about 4–5% on the day.
- **SK hynix's record Q3** (10-28): revenue 104.62 trillion won (about $78.1B, +328% year over year), 80% operating margin — a record — net profit 39.78 trillion won (about $29.7B, +216% year over year, -58% sequentially on a Kioxia base). DRAM prices up a high-20% range sequentially. Over half of 2027 and 2028 output already under long-term contracts, 14 customers on roughly five-year deals. Demand exceeding supply expected through 2030. HBM4 to pass HBM3E in Q4; shareholder returns about $75.4B.
- **UK Autumn Budget data centre covenant** (10-28): Chancellor Healey's speech (about 12:30 GMT). Local investment, jobs, and water protection in exchange for priority grid connections and, where needed, permission for on-site generation. A sovereignty frame keeps sensitive public and military data in Britain. AI Challenge Prize, worker adaptation funding, £10M per year for jobs and industry research. Confirms the October 24 roughly £600M pre-Budget package. OBR forecasts extended to 2031/32 for the first time.

## Insights

- **Supply-chain diversification day**: OpenAI's TPU review, Oracle's multi-chip shelf, and SK hynix's long-term contracts all say the same thing — single-supplier dependence is now priced as risk.
- **Terms, not price tags**: the GSA 40% discount and the UK grid covenant are both news about terms — pay for what you use, grid order as currency. The weight of AI competition is shifting from chip performance to procurement and infrastructure terms.
- **Reports vs announcements, second chapter**: the TPU purchase is report-stage (medium) while the rest are announcements or results (high). Recording different confidence levels on the same day is what keeps the wiki trustworthy.
