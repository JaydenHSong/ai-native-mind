# 2026-05-06 PM Learning Journal — three coordinate axes of harness research

## Start here

The morning ingest focused on self-evolution and observability. This PM follow-up widened the frame and organized harness research into three questions:
- **descriptive**: what do real harness projects look like?
- **prescriptive**: how do we constrain behavior in safety-critical settings?
- **tooling**: what kind of meta-layer can help design and evolve harnesses?

## Main synthesis

- A descriptive taxonomy grounded the field in actual project patterns.
- The CAAF line emphasized deterministic harness behavior and protection against sycophantic compliance.
- The Stanford meta-harness line pushed toward a higher-order layer for constructing and adapting harnesses.

## What changed in the wiki

- Strengthened [[concepts/harness-engineering]].
- Reconnected harness design to [[concepts/mcp]], [[concepts/agent-supply-chain-security]], and [[patterns/llm-wiki]].
- Used this entry as a bridge from the morning’s operational frame to a more structured research map.

## Why it mattered

This entry helped the wiki stop treating harness research as one continuous stream. It introduced a coordinate system that later became useful for organizing many May papers.

## Follow-up

- Keep classifying new harness papers by coordinate, not only by topic.
- Keep asking whether a result is describing reality, prescribing design, or proposing tooling.
