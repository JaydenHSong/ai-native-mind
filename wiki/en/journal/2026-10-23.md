# 2026-10-23 Learning Journal

## What I learned today

- **Google Cloud Agent Compute Engine** (10-22): hardware-isolated microVM per agent session. Harness ("brain") outside the sandbox, sandbox ("hands") with zero credentials — tokens injected onto the wire by a hardware layer. The fourth cloud this year to converge on microVM isolation.
- **Anthropic donates Agent Skills to the Agentic AI Foundation** (10-22): the SKILL.md standard goes vendor-neutral under the Linux Foundation, following MCP. Security-guidance roadmap (backdrop: 76 malicious skills found in a ClawHub sample).
- **NVIDIA × Kazakhstan sovereign AI** (10-22/23): 400 H200 + 480 B300 deployed, 1,000+ Vera Rubin planned for 2027, Firebird's US export license for 45,000 Grace Blackwell GPUs.
- **Lava traces 32 B300s to China-linked endpoints** (10-22): visible via unauthenticated DCGM Exporter exposure. "Does not prove a violation" caveat; CVE-2026-47483 (CVSS 8.2) disclosed alongside. NVIDIA downplays it as "insignificant."
- **Italy's Legislative Decree 179/2026 in force** (10-23): first EU state to embed AI safety in occupational-safety docs (DVR). Solely-automated dismissal decisions banned (breach = void); fines up to €35M or 7% of turnover.
- **OpenAI → Newsom SB 53 letter** (10-20): six asks incl. mandatory kill switches, annual independent audits, automated-AI-research rules. A reversal from its earlier opposition; landed right after the 10-21 misalignment report.
- **Reinforcement**: [[concepts/ai-debt-boom-cracking]] — Nebius $3.5B Citi bridge (SOFR+2.75%, 364 days), CoreWeave CDS ~1,194.5bp.

## Insights

- **The day of hardware enforcement**: ACE (credential injection in the hardware layer) and OpenShell (silicon-level enforcement) landed in the same week — agent security is moving from "software policy" to "hardware enforcement." The wiki's agent-safety-runtime line now has substance.
- **Neutralization of standards, two in a row**: MCP → Agent Skills. The "connectivity" and "knowledge" of the agent stack are both under the Linux Foundation — pack knowledge as skills and it becomes reusable everywhere.
- **Regulatory acceleration on both sides of the Atlantic**: Italy (in force) + California (strengthening lobby) on the same day. The "human decision gate" is statutory in one EU state and a big-lab ask in the US — the same design problem as Duet's approval-gate UX, with legal force.
- **Legal and illicit chip routes in the same day's news**: Kazakhstan's 45,000-unit license vs China's 32-unit suspected leak — compute governance must be read on two axes: "the politics of allocation" and "the reality of enforcement."
