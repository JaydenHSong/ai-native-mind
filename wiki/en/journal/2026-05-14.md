# 2026-05-14 Learning Journal — above-the-model layer

## Start here

This day asked the same question at three moments above the model:
- during learning
- during harness formalization
- during realistic long-horizon evaluation

## Main synthesis

- **Orchestration traces + RL** treated coordination itself as a learnable target.
- **Harness formalization / runtime substrate** clarified responsibilities above the base model.
- **WildClawBench** exposed the ceiling in production-like long-horizon environments.
