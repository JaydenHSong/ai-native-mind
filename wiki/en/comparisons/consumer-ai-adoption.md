# Consumer AI Adoption (a16z Top 100, Oct 2026)

## One-line definition

a16z's 7th Top 100 Gen AI Consumer Apps — the quarterly scorecard of the consumer AI market. ChatGPT's commanding lead, Claude's climb to #3 on web.

## Key data

- ChatGPT: #1 on both web and mobile. 1B+ monthly active mobile users.
- Claude: #3 on web at ~1B monthly visits. Neck-and-neck with Gemini on paid subscribers.
- ChatGPT has 3x more U.S. consumer paid subscribers than Claude or Gemini. As of August, only 4.5% of U.S. consumers held a paid personal subscription.
- Nearly half of U.S. consumers have tried AI; only 25% use it daily. The top 10% of spenders account for roughly half of all consumer AI spending (coding, productivity, creative tools).

## Why it matters

The demand-side data for [[comparisons/frontier-lab-economics]]: whether "pricing power" converts into actual subscriptions. A 4.5% paid-conversion rate is still an early market — most users sit on free tiers. The top 10% carrying half the spend means revenue concentrates in power users (coding, creative). Claude at ~1B web visits shows Anthropic's consumer penetration accelerating — read alongside the 10/1 IPO coverage. Solo-dev lens: the consumer AI app market is still in "habit formation" (25% daily use) — usage-based pricing may beat subscriptions in this structure.
