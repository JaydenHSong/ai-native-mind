## Checkpoint Rules (Important)

- **One Chapter at a Time**: Do not push through multiple chapters on the same day.
- **Deliverables over Passive Reading**: Focus on "What did I produce?" rather than "What did I read?"
- **Fall Back on Difficulty**: If a chapter is too hard, go back to the previous chapter link to rebuild fundamentals.
- **Progress over Perfection**: Build v1 documents/checklists first, and iteratively polish them in subsequent rounds.
