# The Replit × Anthropic Split Dispute — the Moment Models Became Swappable Infrastructure

## Start here

**Analogy**: like moving cloud servers from AWS to GCP. Replit's CEO announced "we moved from Anthropic's servers to OpenAI's" — while insiders counter "we're still on AWS." The core point: **models have become swappable infrastructure** — and the swap itself is now a contested public event.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **VBench** | Replit's internal benchmark — claims to measure vibe-coded app build performance |
| **Provider switching** | Changing model providers — on cost, benchmark, and partnership grounds |

## One-line definition

The case of a coding-agent product (Replit) publicly declaring a model-provider switch on **cost, benchmark, and partnership** grounds — while the CEO's statement, an insider rebuttal, and the September blog's Claude deployment notes contradict each other: **facts in dispute**.

## Key points

- **Statement** (2026-10-16, TechCrunch Disrupt stage): CEO and co-founder Amjad Masad — Replit is 'no longer working' with Anthropic. Three reasons: **cost, partnership, performance**.
- **Quote**: 'I think ChatGPT is better now... consistently, Sol and Astra perform better on building vibe-coded applications from scratch'.
- **Claimed basis**: internal VBench plus public benchmarks.
- **Background**: the August 2026 Replit × OpenAI partnership (Free Mode powered by GPT-5.6 Luna).
- **Rebuttal**: an anonymous insider told TechCrunch the partnership is 'still in place' — **directly conflicting**.
- **Deployment contradiction**: a late-September Replit blog lists **Claude Fable 5/5.1** in Replit Agent production. Masad claimed Replit was one of Anthropic's **top five customers**.
- **Financial context**: enterprise contracts at ~15% of revenue (vs ~2% a year ago), B2B at 50%+, NRR above 400%.
- TechCrunch is awaiting further clarification.

## Why it matters (solo-developer lens)

1. **Models = swappable infrastructure**: the selection criterion is no longer "brand loyalty" but a **function of cost, benchmarks, and partnerships** — routing design ([[patterns/ai-cost-management]]) moves to the center of product strategy.
2. **The benchmark war for vibe-coded apps**: "building from scratch" performance decides provider choice — the evaluation axis for coding agents shifts from code completeness to **product completeness**.
3. **The gap between announcement and deployment**: the CEO's declaration and actual production (Claude Fable 5/5.1) may differ — don't take provider announcements at face value; verify actual routing.

## Limits (stated explicitly)

- **FACTS IN DISPUTE** — the CEO's statement, the insider rebuttal, and the September blog's deployment notes conflict. TechCrunch is awaiting further clarification. This page records **contested claims**, not settled facts.
- VBench is a Replit-internal benchmark — no independent verification possible.
