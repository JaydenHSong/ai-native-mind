# Cohere's Series E — Up to C$7B, with Canada as a Shareholder

## TL;DR

On October 29, 2026, Cohere announced the initial close of a Series E targeting up to C$7 billion (about US$5 billion) at a C$35.7 billion valuation. The same day, Prime Minister Mark Carney announced that the Government of Canada will invest up to C$1.6 billion in equity, paid in stages through the Canada Development Investment Corporation, tied to jobs and domestic compute commitments.

## Key facts

- **Round**: initial close announced; target up to C$7B. Germany's Schwarz Group leads; new investors include the Government of Canada, Bell Ventures, and Bullhound Capital; NVIDIA, Inovia Capital, and Prosperity7 Ventures return. The amount actually secured at initial close is undisclosed, though the company says most of the target is secured.
- **Government equity**: up to C$1.6B (about US$1.1B) as shares, not a grant, invested in stages with regular reporting on commitments. The final ownership percentage is undisclosed.
- **Three conditions**: Cohere keeps its Canadian incorporation and headquarters, creates at least 1,000 Canadian technical jobs by 2030, and invests at least C$1.7B in additional Canadian computing infrastructure by 2030. The deal sits on top of a C$240M commitment under Canada's 2024 Sovereign AI Compute Strategy.
- **Company metrics**: annual recurring revenue grew 165% year over year to September 2026, with about 300 customers (no dollar figure disclosed). Reports also say a merger with Germany's Aleph Alpha is in its final stages.
- **Record**: if filled, the largest private financing ever by a Canadian technology company.

## Why it matters

1. **A sovereign-AI equity model**: the state enters as a shareholder, not a grant-maker. This could become the template for how governments own a stake in frontier AI.
2. **Conditional capital as precedent**: jobs, infrastructure, and headquarters conditions written into an investment agreement are a reference table for future public-money AI deals.
3. **Read closes carefully**: a target (C$7B) is not cash received. Headline round sizes in funding news routinely blur that distinction.

## Limitations

- The initial-close amount and the government's final ownership percentage are undisclosed; C$7B is a target.
- ARR is disclosed only as a growth rate, without an absolute figure.
- The Aleph Alpha merger is at the reporting stage, not a confirmed company announcement.
