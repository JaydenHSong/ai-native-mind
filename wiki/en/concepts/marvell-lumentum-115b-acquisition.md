# Marvell–Lumentum $115B Acquisition — Optical Interconnect Consolidation for AI Data Centers

## Start here

**Analogy**: rebuilding the roads inside a data center from copper wire to light. As servers multiply, road width becomes the bottleneck.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Optical interconnect** | Moving data between servers with light |
| **All-stock deal** | An acquisition paid in the buyer's shares, not cash |

## One-line definition

Agreed 2026-11-02: Marvell acquires Lumentum at an enterprise value of about $115B in an all-stock deal, combining custom silicon and electrical connectivity with optical products.

## Key facts

- **Terms**: a fixed exchange ratio of 4.5 Marvell shares per Lumentum share, about $1,132 per share on 10-28 closing prices, a premium of about 23.9%. Marvell's existing shareholders keep about 67% on a fully diluted basis.
- **Logic**: moving data between servers is becoming the bottleneck in AI data centers, and the architecture is shifting from electrical to optical connectivity, Marvell says. It claims a $550B addressable market after the merger and more than $1B in annual run-rate cost synergies.
- **Market reaction**: on announcement day, Marvell fell 12.72%, Lumentum 18.59%, and peer Ciena 11.34%. Dilution concerns, cycle timing and regulatory review were cited as caution factors. Closing is expected in the first half of 2028, with review alone possibly taking 14 to 20 months.

## Why it matters (solo-developer view)

1. **The AI race spread from models to interconnect**: the technology linking chips now decides deal sizes.
2. **The market is not cheering mega-deals**: strategic logic and share prices moved in opposite directions on announcement day.
3. **Regulation is the schedule**: a close more than a year out means this is not something to build a product roadmap around yet.

## Limitations (stated)

- Synergy and market-size figures are company claims without independent verification.
- Breakup fees apply: $3.2B if regulators block the deal, up to $5.8B in other scenarios.
