# Anthropic IPO Prices Tonight — Offering Price Expected Above $80, ANT Lists Nov 19

## Start here

**Analogy**: a company is about to sell its shares to the public for the first time. It originally said "we will sell at $70–78 per share," but so many people want to buy that reports say it may price above $80. **The final price was set at $83 on the evening of November 18 — see [[concepts/anthropic-ipo-priced-83-ant-trading]] for the confirmed terms.**

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **IPO (initial public offering)** | The first time a private company sells shares and lists on a stock exchange |
| **Offering price** | The price at which shares are first sold in an IPO |
| **Ticker** | The short symbol for a company on the stock market — Anthropic's is ANT |
| **S-1** | The registration statement filed with the U.S. SEC for a listing — the official document carrying the company's finances and structure |
| **Run-rate** | Recent revenue annualized — "at this pace, this much in a year" |
| **Class F shares** | Special founder shares that keep majority voting control with a small equity stake |

## One-line definition

Anthropic will set its IPO price on the evening of November 18 and begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on November 19. The official offering range is **$70–78** per share (825 million shares; $64.35B raised at $78), but the WSJ reports a price **above $80**, and Bloomberg reports demand at about **7x** the offering. **Confirmed follow-up (11-19): the price was set at $83 — [[concepts/anthropic-ipo-priced-83-ant-trading]]**

## Key points

- **Schedule**: pricing on 2026-11-18 in the evening (U.S. Eastern), first trading 2026-11-19 on the Nasdaq Global Select Market, ticker ANT.
- **Terms**: per S-1 Amendment No. 3 (filed 2026-11-17), 825 million Class A shares at $70–78 per share. Company sale of 801,351,354 shares + existing-shareholder sale of 23,648,646 shares. About $64.35B at $78. Separately, Google, NVIDIA, AMD, Microsoft, Broadcom, Micron, TSMC, and others will join a $26B concurrent private placement of Class N shares at the IPO price.
- **Demand**: per Bloomberg (11-18), orders are about 7x the offering (up from ~6x mid-roadshow). Altimeter Capital's Brad Gerstner said he requested a $5B allocation. The WSJ (11-17), citing sources, reported a price above $80, and advisers are discussing a $1.5T–$1.6T valuation (fully diluted valuation at the top of the range is ~$1.4T).
- **Financials**: annualized revenue run-rate of about $81B as of October 31, 2026. Q3 2026 revenue of ~$17B per the S-1 (+47% QoQ, slowing from +144% in Q2). Multi-year computing and infrastructure commitments of about $900.9B.
- **Structure**: founders' Class F shares keep 50.1% of voting power; suppliers becoming shareholders at the same time is a distinctive feature. Up to 20% of the offering is to be allocated to retail investors.
- **Confirmed follow-up**: the above-$80 price was a report, but the final price was set at $83 per share on the evening of November 18 (with the offering upsized to 980 million shares). See [[concepts/anthropic-ipo-priced-83-ant-trading]] for the confirmed terms.

## Why it matters (solo-developer lens)

1. **The funding map for frontier labs changes**: suppliers (Google, NVIDIA, AMD, Microsoft, and others) putting in $26B to become shareholders at the same time means "the people who sell chips" and "the people who build models" are tied together by capital. Read with the $895.4B in commitments in [[concepts/anthropic-s1a-q3-revenue-commitments]], it shows how large Anthropic's growth funding lines are.
2. **The tension between slowing growth and commitments**: Q3 revenue growth slowed from +144% to +47% while computing commitments stand at about $901B. From an [[concepts/llm-evaluation]] angle, that gap — earning more slowly while the money it must spend grows — is the key analytical point of this listing.
3. **A reversal of roadshow expectations**: on November 8, reports said valuation expectations had fallen from $2T to $1–1.75T ([[concepts/anthropic-ipo-roadshow-valuation-cut]]); ten days later the mood flipped to 7x demand and an above-$80 price outlook. A classic late-IPO pattern where demand reports pull the price up.
4. **Governance**: founders keep 50.1% of voting power through Class F — control stays with the founders after listing. How independence and safety governance are maintained is something to watch alongside the safety work in [[concepts/anthropic-anvil-automated-red-teaming]].

## Limits (stated explicitly)

- This page records the outlook just before pricing; the final price was set at $83 ([[concepts/anthropic-ipo-priced-83-ant-trading]]). Do not read the confirmed price from this page's outlook figures.
- Q3 revenue of ~$17B and an $81B run-rate are S-1 figures that effectively match the S-1/A's $16.96B — no contradiction.
- confidence is medium — an S-1 amendment as a primary source crossed with WSJ and Bloomberg reporting.
