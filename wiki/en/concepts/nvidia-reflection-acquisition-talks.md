# Nvidia × Reflection AI Acquisition/Investment Talks (FT 2026-10-10)

## One-line definition

An FT scoop reporting that Nvidia is in talks to acquire Reflection AI or expand its investment — the chip company expanding up the stack (infrastructure → models).

## Key points

- **Report**: FT scoop 10/10 via Reuters. Ongoing follow-up coverage through 10/11–12 (PYMNTS and others).
- **Talks**: acquisition or expanded investment, early stage. Agreement possible within weeks, but collapse is also possible.
- **Candidate structures**: full acquisition / acqui-hire (hiring key talent + licensing technology to dodge antitrust review) / larger equity stake / expanded compute supply.
- **Stake**: Nvidia is already one of the largest shareholders with $800M invested.
- **Valuation**: raising at $25B pre-money (April basis; CEO Misha Laskin via CNBC). Last October it raised $2B at an $8B valuation — 3x in a year.
- **Company**: founded 2024 by ex-DeepMind researchers Misha Laskin and Ioannis Antonoglou. Open-weight coding/agent models. Beam launched 10/5.
- **Background**: the Trump administration wants "U.S.-built open-weights" as a DeepSeek counterweight. DOJ's Groq probe makes a full acquisition an antitrust burden.

## Why it matters

This negotiation is part of a structural trend: **chip companies expanding up the stack**. Two weeks after AMD's $8.2B World Labs acquisition, Nvidia moves to secure an open-weights lab. Takeaways for the AI-native programmer: first, acqui-hire is becoming the standard structure for dodging antitrust review — read AI M&A news as "acquisition or acqui-hire" first. Second, the administration's desire for "U.S.-built open-weights" as a DeepSeek counterweight means a geopolitical premium on open-weight models — more capital will flow into the open-model ecosystem.
