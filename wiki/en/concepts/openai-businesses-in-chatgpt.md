# OpenAI Opens Businesses in ChatGPT Beta — Business Hub and Business Agents (Nov 10)

## Start here

**Analogy**: until now, ChatGPT was a guide who told a passer-by "that shop over there is good." Now the shop owner can hang their own sign next to the guide, correct their prices and opening hours — and when a customer asks, the shop's own clerk (an agent) steps out to answer.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Business Hub** | The dashboard where a business manages its profile inside ChatGPT |
| **Business Agent** | A branded chatbot a business publishes — it answers product comparisons and returns questions |
| **Claim** | The process by which a business verifies ownership of its ChatGPT listing and gains control over it |

## One-line definition

On 2026-11-10, OpenAI opened Businesses in ChatGPT in beta. Businesses can claim and manage their profile inside ChatGPT, view engagement metrics in Business Hub, and — if they are eligible US ecommerce or online-service businesses — publish a Business Agent, a branded chatbot. ChatGPT is moving from a search and discovery channel toward a commerce channel.

## Key facts

- **Business Hub**: a business claims its organisation in ChatGPT and, once verified, updates key details such as its website, description, logo, delivery and returns policies, and adds product feeds with prices and availability. It shows aggregated metrics for the past 28 days — mention counts, website clicks and discovery topics — in Brand insights and Agent insights tabs, with 7-day and 28-day views. The first live dashboards carried data through November 9 (UTC), a day's lag.
- **Business Agents**: a branded chatbot a business publishes. Users can open it from the business profile to compare products or ask about returns, and it answers from the business's information. Its policy requires it to be clear that it is AI, not to pose as a human or an independent adviser, and not to create false urgency or keep pushing a sale after a customer says no. At launch it is limited to eligible US ecommerce and online-service businesses with a website. It cannot yet complete a purchase, a booking or an order lookup.
- **Advertising link**: a business can sponsor its Business Agent and use it as the destination for ChatGPT Ads, with sponsorship labelled. The hub and agents are free for now (Digiday, from a briefing with OpenAI monetisation VP Benji Shomair); charging later is not ruled out.
- **Privacy and ranking**: OpenAI says it does not share users' conversations or Business Agent chats with businesses, and metrics are aggregated and shown only above a volume threshold. Claiming a profile or advertising does not change ChatGPT's independent answers, according to OpenAI. Verification can use a business phone number, email, a DNS TXT record or a Shopify store; a blue check appears only after Persona identity verification.
- **Launch context**: the same day as Anthropic's Claude workplace bundle, so both companies competed over the business and workplace surface on the same day. It follows the November 9 launch of ChatGPT Enterprise on AWS Marketplace — two consecutive business moves.

## Why it matters (solo-developer view)

1. **ChatGPT is becoming a place to find and ask a shop, not just to get answers**: for a solo business or small shop, ChatGPT is a new discovery channel. Claiming a profile lets the business control how its facts appear in answers.
2. **The boundary between ads and independent answers is now a practical issue**: how sponsored agents and independent answers are labelled and policed will shape whether users keep trusting ChatGPT.
3. **Agents cannot transact yet — that is the limit and the next step**: today they handle enquiries. The moment purchases, bookings and order lookups attach, the real competition in agentic commerce begins.

## Limitations (stated)

- Business Agents cannot yet complete a purchase. Claiming a profile does not guarantee higher placement or more sales.
- The schedule for offering agents outside the US is undisclosed; when a Korean business can publish one is unknown.
- Metrics are aggregated and appear only above a volume threshold, so small businesses may wait before seeing any figures.
