# Oracle's $20B Raise and the Nov 2 AI-Spending Selloff — Nasdaq Down 1.07%

## Start here

**Analogy**: a company says it will expand a factory by raising both debt and new shares at once, and shareholders first ask whether that factory will ever pay for itself.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Debt-and-equity raise** | Raising funds by borrowing (bonds) and diluting (new shares) at the same time |

## One-line definition

2026-11-02: Oracle disclosed plans to raise about $20B — $10B in bonds plus $10B in stock — for AI and cloud expansion, and its shares fell about 8.8%, leading a tech selloff.

## Key facts

- **Raise**: a $10B bond offering plus a $10B common-stock sale. Stated uses include AI infrastructure capex, debt repayment, investments, acquisitions, dividends and buybacks.
- **Market**: Oracle fell about 8.85% to $119.10. Marvell fell 12.72%, Lumentum 18.59% and Intel 8.8% the same day. The Nasdaq closed down 1.07% (25,164.32), the S&P 500 down 0.51% (7,541.40), the Dow up 0.41% (51,983.98).
- **Background**: the session extended October's Nasdaq selloff of 5.3%. Investors questioned whether massive AI spending will generate sufficient returns, and whether debt-funded buildouts are sustainable while borrowing costs stay elevated. Pre-election caution the day before the midterms added to the mood.
- **Contrast**: S&P 500 third-quarter earnings were on track to grow more than 30% year on year, with AI-linked companies accounting for much of the increase — yet the financing method of AI expansion still drove selling.

## Why it matters (solo-developer view)

1. **The AI infrastructure debate moved from 'how much' to 'how funded'**: even good earnings do not shield a debt-and-dilution expansion from selling.
2. **It links directly to the wiki's AI debt-boom page**: after CoreWeave's $50B debt and spiking CDS, Oracle joins the same narrative.
3. **One company's filing moved a whole sector**: a single raise announcement dragged semiconductor and networking names down with it.

## Limitations (stated)

- The eventual size and timing of the raise depend on execution after disclosure.
- Anthropic quarterly revenue and future spending-commitment figures carried in the same TradingKey article were not included here: they do not reconcile with the S-1's first-half $16.3B and need separate verification.
