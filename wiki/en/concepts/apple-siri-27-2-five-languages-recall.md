# Apple 27.2: Siri AI Adds Five Languages and Conversation Recall

## TL;DR

On 2026-10-26 Apple shipped iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, tvOS, visionOS, and HomePod 27.2. Siri AI gains conversation recall, broader personal-context sources, and an Excluded Apps control, plus five new languages: French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish. Siri AI remains unavailable in the EU and mainland China, and the features require iPhone 16 or later, or iPhone 15 Pro/Max.

## Key facts

- **Conversation recall**: Siri AI retains useful context from prior conversations, so users can ask about things previously discussed, such as a restaurant it recommended last week.
- **Personal-context expansion**: answers can draw on photos attached in Messages, Calendar event attendees, voicemails, and improved Safari history and Reading List results.
- **Control**: the new Excluded Apps setting lets users stop Siri, Search, and suggestions from accessing a particular app. Granular text selection allows copying part of a Siri response; watchOS 27.2 gets faster responses.
- **Other 27.2 items**: contextual Suggestions in Messages, SynthID watermark support in Photos, Apple TV profiles, and Apple Pencil support plus Edge Light on the iPhone Duo.
- **Languages and regions**: French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish join English. Siri AI is still not available in the European Union or mainland China.
- **Devices and deferrals**: Siri AI features are limited to all iPhone 16 models and later plus iPhone 15 Pro/Pro Max; other iPhones receive only the non-Siri changes. The redesigned Health app was removed from the final build and deferred to a later point release.

## Why it matters

1. **Memory is the product**: assistant competition is shifting from model quality to how well the assistant remembers the user — memory design is product design.
2. **Access paired with a kill switch**: broader personal-context access ships with Excluded Apps — Apple's answer is to sell the control alongside the access.
3. **Directly relevant to Korean-speaking users**: Korean is among the five new languages, so this update is immediately tangible rather than abstract news.

## Limitations (explicit)

- Siri AI is unavailable in the EU and mainland China, and older iPhones are excluded from the Siri features.
- Retention and deletion policies for conversation recall were not clearly detailed in the release notes.
- Announced features can be cut from final builds, as the Health redesign deferral shows.
