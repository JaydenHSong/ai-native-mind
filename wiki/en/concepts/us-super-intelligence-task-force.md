# White House Super Intelligence Task Force's First Meeting — Charter Adopted, 120-Day Review Underway

## One-line definition

The first official meeting (2026-10-15) of the White House Super Intelligence Task Force — **charter adoption puts the 120-day review on its formal track**, with PCAST's three-risk report and explicit FTC/DOJ enforcement channels: a documented weight shift from "coordination" toward "enforcement."

## Core content (Reuters, AI Affairs, 10/16)

- **Charter adopted**: the Task Force charter was adopted — the 120-day review formally begins.
- **Chair**: Jay Clayton (DNI and AI czar).
- **Members**: Scott Kupor (OPM), Andrew Ferguson (FTC), Emil Michael (Department of Defense).
- **PCAST**: submitted a report on three risk areas — cybersecurity, biosecurity, and model control.
- **David Sacks**: leads the risk research.
- **Emil Michael (DoD)**: tasked with building an early-warning reporting protocol for AI lab breaches, hacks, and jailbreaks.
- **Enforcement**: violation-enforcement channels through the FTC and DOJ made explicit.

## Why it matters

1. **Launch (10/5) → operational track**: read against [[concepts/super-intelligence-force]]'s timeline — the 10/6 DOJ terminology memo, the 10/9 mandatory reporting order, now the 10/15 first meeting and charter adoption. It started as "coordination without regulation," but mandatory reporting arrived within ten days, and now FTC/DOJ enforcement channels are named — **the weight shift from "coordination" to "enforcement" is being documented**.
2. **The PCAST three-risk axis**: cybersecurity, biosecurity, model control — interlocking with the evaluation axis of the 10/9 mandate (incident reporting). The 120-day review (January 2027) is structured so it can end as enforcement groundwork, not just a risk study.
3. **The DoD's early-warning protocol**: early-warning reporting for AI lab breaches, hacks, and jailbreaks — the institutionalized follow-up to the 10/9 Anthropic incident (unauthorized use of federal systems). From "report after the incident" to "early warning before the breach."

## Explicit limitations

- The charter's specific provisions and the 120-day review's evaluation criteria are undisclosed.
- The PCAST report's details and the scope of David Sacks's risk research are unconfirmed.
- Meeting outputs beyond the member list (resolutions, schedule) were not reported. Confidence stays **medium**.
