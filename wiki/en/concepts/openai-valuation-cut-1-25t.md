# OpenAI Cuts Valuation Target to $1.25T After Investor Pushback

## TL;DR

OpenAI cut the valuation target on a funding round of up to $30B to $1.25T, down from $1.4T, after investor resistance, the Financial Times reported on 2026-10-26. Morgan Stanley and Dragoneer had offered the company to private-wealth clients at a $1.4T pre-money valuation, but demand was weaker than in earlier rounds. Investors have until October 30 to commit. At $1.25T the company would still be worth about 50% more than the $852B valuation set in March.

## Key facts

- **The cut**: $1.4T → $1.25T, a $150B reduction. CNBC first reported the $1.25T figure; the FT confirmed the $150B shift. The valuation excludes the new capital raised.
- **Weak demand**: documents seen by the FT show Morgan Stanley and Dragoneer offered OpenAI to the bank's private-wealth clients at $1.4T pre-money; people familiar with the process said client demand was weaker than in earlier rounds, with some investors already heavily invested in prior raises.
- **Timeline and size**: prospective investors have until October 30 to commit, per the FT; the company may raise less than $30B depending on interest. Final size unconfirmed.
- **Lead candidates**: Coatue Management and UAE funds are in discussions to lead, per CNBC. Bloomberg reported talks with BlackRock. Reuters reported on 2026-10-19 that SoftBank, Abu Dhabi-based MGX, and other existing backers were in talks to join.
- **Notable term**: the FT reported the deal gives OpenAI a right of first refusal on new computing power developed by its UAE partners — fundraising and compute procurement bundled into one deal.
- **Comparison point**: the March round raised $122B at an $852B valuation. OpenAI declined to comment to Bloomberg and the FT; Coatue did not respond.

## Why it matters

1. **Pricing power gets tested**: frontier-lab valuations are now being set by buyer response, not by the ask — a thermometer for the AI boom.
2. **Capital and compute bundled**: a right of first refusal on a partner's future compute inside a financing deal — money and GPUs in one contract.
3. **The IPO race**: OpenAI's financing and Anthropic's expected IPO are pricing off each other — one side's terms become the other's benchmark.

## Limitations (explicit)

- Report-based figures; neither OpenAI nor Coatue confirmed them.
- Final valuation and size settle only after the October 30 deadline — current numbers are targets at the negotiation stage.
- The UAE right-of-first-refusal term comes from FT reporting; the underlying documents are not public.
