# Character.AI Bankruptcy — Disney's $20M Stalking-Horse Bid

## Start here

**Analogy**: a beloved app with 20 million monthly users selling for 1/50 of its peak valuation in three years. The equation "many users = money" broke — and the final blow was teen-safety litigation.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Chapter 11** | US Bankruptcy Code Chapter 11 — reorganization bankruptcy; the business keeps operating while a sale or restructuring proceeds |
| **Stalking-horse bid** | The first bid in a sale auction, setting the benchmark price — Disney's $20M is the floor |
| **Non-exclusive perpetual license** | A right to use specific IP forever, without exclusivity — the substance Disney is after |

## One-line definition

AI companion app Character.AI (Character Technologies) filed for Chapter 11 on 2026-10-12, with Disney stepping in as the $20M stalking-horse bidder — from an implied ~$2.5B value to a $20M bid, the collapse curve of consumer-AI unit economics.

## Key content

### The filing (2026-10-12)

- Reuters first reported; CNN followed up on 10/13. Assets and liabilities on the filing: **$50M–$100M** each.
- The sale is expected **late November–early December 2026**. The company keeps operating, but if no operator-buyer emerges it faces **wind-down**.
- Disney (Disney Worldwide Services)'s stalking-horse bid of **$20M**: a **non-exclusive perpetual license** to specific IP + **transition services** from former Character.AI employees Disney hired.

### The valuation-collapse timeline

| When | Status |
|------|--------|
| 2021 | Founded by Noam Shazeer and Daniel de Freitas |
| 2023-03 | **$150M round at ~$1B valuation** |
| 2024 | Google's license-and-hire deal (**~$2.7B**) — both founders return to Google |
| Mid-2024 | MAU peak **~28M** |
| 2026-10 | MAU **~20M**, bankruptcy filing, Disney bid **$20M** |

### The shadow of teen-safety litigation

- In the background: **teen-safety litigation** — Megan Garcia's lawsuit over her son Sewell Setzer III's suicide, plus **5 settlements** earlier this year.
- CNN: CEO Karandeep Anand **joined Disney as CTO last month** — the executive exodus was already underway before the filing.

## Why it matters

1. **The "MAU = value" equation breaks**: a 20M-MAU consumer AI still hits Chapter 11 when monetization fails and litigation costs pile up. The opposite of [[comparisons/frontier-lab-economics]]'s "pricing-power infrastructure" — when unit economics don't work, bankruptcy is the endpoint.
2. **Teen safety as an existential risk**: [[concepts/ai-teen-safety-evaluation]]'s 'Unacceptable Risk' verdict (quantitative evaluation) and the courts' lawsuits (legal risk) land in the same week in a single bankruptcy file. Advertising "protection features" for teen-facing products is itself legal exposure surface.
3. **The talent leaves first**: the founders already returned to Google in 2024 — what remains is IP and users. The AI-startup dissolution pattern where "people + models" exit before the "company."
