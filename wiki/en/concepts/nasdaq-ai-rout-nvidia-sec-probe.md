# Nasdaq Drops 5.28% — AI Stocks Lose $4T in a Week, NVIDIA Falls 14% on Report of SEC Accounting Probe (Nov 20)

## Start here

**Analogy**: the brakes slammed on the AI stock rally. In a single day the Nasdaq fell more than 5%, giving back everything it had gained this year, and bellwether NVIDIA slid 14% after a report — just before the close — that the securities regulator is looking into its accounting. Newly listed Anthropic fell toward its offering price too. Doubts about whether pouring money into AI actually pays off were priced in, in a single day.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Nasdaq** | The US stock index dominated by technology shares — heavily weighted toward AI stocks |
| **Correction** | A fall of 10% or more from a peak — the S&P 500 entered one |
| **SEC** | The US Securities and Exchange Commission — the agency that supervises listed companies' accounting and disclosure |
| **Greenshoe** | The underwriters' right to buy and sell extra shares when demand is strong |
| **VIX** | The market's fear index — when it rises, investors are nervous |

## One-line definition

On November 20, 2026, US stocks plunged on an AI sell-off. The Nasdaq fell **5.28% to 22,617.73**, erasing its entire 2026 gain, and the S&P 500 fell **3.70% to 6,910.39** — a seventh straight daily decline and a correction, 11.6% below its October 6 all-time high. NVIDIA closed **down 14.01% at $125.53** after a late-session Reuters report that the SEC is examining its accounting practices, and Anthropic fell **8.5% to $88.91** on its second day of trading, coming within 5 cents of its $83 offering price. For the week, the S&P fell 8.6%, the Nasdaq 10.0% and the Dow 8.0% — the worst week since March 2020.

## Key points

- **Indices**: Nasdaq −5.28% (about −2.7% year to date), S&P 500 −3.70% (seventh straight decline), Dow −1.93% to 48,147.33. Weekly losses: S&P −8.6%, Nasdaq −10.0%, Dow −8.0% — the worst week since March 2020.
- **NVIDIA**: −14.01% to a $125.53 close, with an intraday low of $124.50, a 52-week low. Down 23% for the week, its worst week since the 2008 financial crisis. About $2.5T in market value erased from the peak (per Motley Fool). Losses deepened late in the session after Reuters reported the SEC is looking into NVIDIA's accounting practices — unconfirmed by both the SEC and NVIDIA. This reported SEC probe is a separate thread from the November 19 California subpoena (see [[concepts/nvidia-california-subpoena-market-rout]]).
- **Other AI stocks**: AMD down in the low teens, Broadcom and Micron down 12–14%, the Philadelphia semiconductor index −10.7%. Nebius −16.1%, CoreWeave −12.0%.
- **Anthropic (ANT)**: down 8.5% to $88.91 on its second day. Its intraday low of $83.05 came within 5 cents of the $83 offering price. About −23% from the day-one high of $115.10. With the greenshoe fully exercised, total proceeds reached about $93.5B, and the IPO closed on November 20.
- **Flight to safety**: the VIX (fear index) surged to around 40 (+41%). The 10-year Treasury yield fell below 5%, and Bitcoin broke below $60,000.
- **Background**: spreading doubts about the profitability of AI investment — slowing data-center spending, the way AI projects are financed, and signs of spending discipline from big tech. Signals of a hawkish Federal Reserve rate stance overlapped. The same day brought President Trump's threat of a tax on overseas data centers (see [[concepts/trump-data-center-tax-threat]]).

## Why it matters (solo-developer lens)

1. **"Is AI a bubble?" started getting priced answers**: the debt and overheating worries in [[concepts/ai-debt-boom-cracking]] moved from an October analysis piece to an actual November 20 share price. A weekly Nasdaq drop of 10% is a loss, not a debate.
2. **Investigation risk now outweighs earnings**: NVIDIA reported record Q3 revenue of $115.8B (+103%) on November 17, yet the stock is being driven by a chain of investigation news — the resignation (Nov 12), the California subpoena (Nov 19), the SEC report (Nov 20). The price is set by "what else will come out," not "how much did it earn."
3. **The IPO's first-day excitement cooled in two days**: Anthropic went from a $111.50 open on day one to an intraday $83.05 on day two. A record raise ($93.5B) and a falling share price coexist in the same week — money is pouring in, but prices are getting scrutinized.

## Limits (stated explicitly)

- The SEC accounting-probe report comes from Reuters and is unconfirmed by both the SEC and NVIDIA. Whether an examination exists, its scope, and its conclusion are all unverified.
- **Watch the ANT close**: Yahoo Finance, Bloomberg, and CNN reported a close of $88.91 (−8.5%), while AP, the Wall Street Journal, and MarketWatch reported $88.30 (−9.1%). Outlets disagree on the closing price, so this page uses $88.91 as the main figure and notes $88.30 alongside it. The confirmed close needs re-checking against the official exchange record.
- **The market-value-erased figures use different baselines**: Motley Fool's "about $2.5T erased from the peak" is a cumulative from-the-peak figure, while [[concepts/nvidia-california-subpoena-market-rout]]'s "about $669B erased over six sessions" covers the six sessions through November 19. The periods and baselines differ, so the two numbers must not be compared directly.
- confidence is medium — Motley Fool, Reuters, and CNBC cross-check, but the SEC report is unconfirmed and outlets disagree on the ANT close.
