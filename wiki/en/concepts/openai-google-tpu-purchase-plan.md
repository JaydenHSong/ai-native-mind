# OpenAI Reportedly Weighs Buying Google TPUs — Testing Its Nvidia Dependence

## TL;DR

On the evening of 2026-10-27, The Information reported (via Reuters) that OpenAI plans to buy Google's TPU chips, citing a person familiar with the matter. No financial terms or scale were disclosed, and neither company confirmed the plan — OpenAI declined to comment and Google did not immediately respond. The report matters for its direction: even the largest Nvidia buyer is reported to be diversifying its silicon supply.

## Key facts

- **The report**: OpenAI plans to buy Google TPUs, according to a single person familiar with the matter cited by The Information. Terms and scale are undisclosed.
- **Staffing clues**: OpenAI has hired Google TPU veterans, including hardware chief Richard Ho, and is recruiting a TPU compiler engineer — the people needed to run a second chip stack are already arriving.
- **Testing the Nvidia commitment**: the plan is reported as testing OpenAI's roughly $100B in Nvidia compute commitments. OpenAI is among the largest buyers of Nvidia GPUs.
- **Deepening Google ties**: last week OpenAI said it would make frontier models available through Google Cloud; Reuters reported in June 2025 that OpenAI had begun renting Google chips, and Google began selling TPUs directly to some customers this year.
- **Market context**: Amazon, Microsoft, and Alphabet together plan up to $600B in AI infrastructure spending this year, while investors question when that spending converts into returns. Alphabet reports earnings later on Wednesday 2026-10-28.

## Why it matters

1. **Diversification signal**: when the biggest buyer diversifies, supplier pricing power shifts — and inference cost curves for smaller developers move with supplier competition.
2. **Report vs announcement discipline**: this item is recorded at confidence medium precisely because it is an unconfirmed report; keeping that distinction is what makes the wiki trustworthy.
3. **Cloud-chip bundling**: a model provider's cloud (Google Cloud) plus its chips (TPUs) makes one vendor's price list a direct input to operating cost — another argument for multi-cloud strategy.

## Limitations

- A single-source report with no company confirmation — confidence medium.
- No scale, timing, or TPU generation disclosed.
- The report does not say whether TPU purchases would reduce Nvidia commitments or add capacity on top.
