# Cat Wu Joins OpenAI — Claude Code Product Lead Departs Anthropic Weeks Before Its IPO

## Start here

**Analogy**: the head of product behind a hit coding tool moving to the rival firm. It is not just a hire — the know-how of how the product is built moves with the person, so the agent race shifts with the talent.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Core Products** | OpenAI's core product organization — ChatGPT, Codex and other flagship products |
| **Claude Code** | Anthropic's AI coding agent tool |

## One-line definition

Announced 2026-11-05: Catherine "Cat" Wu, Anthropic's product lead for Claude Code and Cowork, is joining OpenAI to lead product management for Core Products — less than a week after her last day at Anthropic on 2026-10-30, and weeks before Anthropic's expected IPO.

## Key facts

- **New role**: head of product management for OpenAI Core Products, reporting into Thibault Sottiaux, who leads Core Products, alongside Nick Turley (enterprise products) and Ashley Alexander (consumer products). Wu's stated priority is building "reliable agents" that keep users "in control" while freeing up their time.
- **Track record**: joined Anthropic in August 2024 in research product management and led Claude Code from its days as a small internal tool to a shipping product. Became head of product for Claude Code in 2025 and later took on Cowork and Tag. She left for Cursor once in 2025 and returned.
- **Timing**: announced her departure on X on 2026-11-03 ("Friday was my last day"), then the OpenAI move on 2026-11-05. Anthropic confirmed the departure and OpenAI confirmed the hire. No successor has been named.
- **Wider departures**: Claude Code engineers Thariq Shihipar and Anthony Morris announced their departures within a day of each other in the same week. Wu is the second departure in that sequence, not the third as first reported.
- **IPO context**: Anthropic filed its S-1 publicly on 2026-10-26 and its amended S-1/A on 2026-11-02 reported Q3 revenue of about $17B. The listing is expected later this month.

## Why it matters (solo-developer view)

1. **The agent race became a talent war**: the contest is no longer only model performance but who builds the agent products. Claude Code's product know-how moving to Codex is direct.
2. **A pre-IPO departure is a signal**: a core product lead leaving right after revenue was confirmed in the S-1/A shows internal dynamics moving independently of the numbers.
3. **"Reliable agents" is the shared language on both sides**: Wu's stated priority sits between Anthropic's safety narrative and OpenAI's agent expansion — a convergence of product philosophy worth reading closely.

## Limitations (stated)

- Wu has not stated her reasons for leaving. Speculation about the IPO, compensation or organization is unconfirmed.
- The exact scope of her authority under Sottiaux (reporting line, products owned) is not confirmed beyond the announcement wording.
