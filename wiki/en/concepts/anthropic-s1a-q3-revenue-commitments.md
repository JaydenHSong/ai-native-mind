# Anthropic S-1/A — Q3 Revenue of $17B and $895B in Compute Commitments Disclosed

## Start here

**Analogy**: revenue grew 12-fold in a year, but the computer-rental commitments stacked up to more than 50 times that revenue. One filing shows both the speed of growth and its price tag.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **S-1/A** | An amendment to the S-1 IPO registration statement |
| **Operating loss** | Revenue minus operating expenses coming out negative |
| **Purchase commitments** | Contracted future spending |

## One-line definition

Filed 2026-11-02: Anthropic's amended IPO filing (S-1/A) disclosed Q3 revenue of $16.96B and about $895.4B in compute and infrastructure commitments, and a swing from operating profit back to an operating loss on short-term compute costs.

## Key facts

- **Q3 revenue**: $16.96B, up 47% from $11.56B in Q2 and about 1,127% from $1.38B a year earlier. Nine-month revenue reached $33.24B, about 12 times the prior-year period.
- **Profitability**: an operating loss of $567M, versus a $974M operating profit in Q2. The $23.29B net loss ($104.04B for nine months) is largely explained by non-cash accounting charges.
- **Compute costs**: $9.86B in Q3, up 77% from Q2, rising from 48% to 58% of revenue. Demand outran capacity bought 12–18 months ahead, so about $5B was bought on short-term commitments at higher prices.
- **Commitment total**: about $895.4B in purchase commitments and leases, mostly compute and infrastructure, running through 2045. About half falls due by the end of 2030, and about $69.8B within 12 months after September 30, against $131.8B in cash and short-term investments.
- **IPO stage**: the price range, share count and deal size remain blank, so pricing has not begun. A Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT is planned.

## Why it matters (solo-developer view)

1. **A frontier lab's unit economics are now public**: compute at 58% of revenue is a baseline for pricing agent services — it caps the margin.
2. **Commitments are strategy**: $895B is both a bet on future demand and fixed cost if demand bends. When reading lab funding news, check the commitment maturity schedule before the revenue headline.
3. **Yesterday's exclusion rule worked**: figures excluded on 11-03 as unreconciled were confirmed via the formal filing, showing the distinction between unverified numbers and disclosed ones.

## Limitations (stated)

- Interim figures are unaudited, and the amendment is not yet effective, so it can change before pricing.
- The non-cash portion of the quarterly net loss was reported without a quarterly breakdown.
- October revenue is absent, so whether Q3 growth continued cannot be read from this filing.
