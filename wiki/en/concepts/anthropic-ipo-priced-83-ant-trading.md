# Anthropic IPO Priced at $83 — 980M Shares, About $81.3B Raised, ANT Lists Nov 19

## Start here

**Analogy**: yesterday the company said "we will sell at $70–78 per share," but so many people wanted to buy that it set the price at **$83**. The number of shares for sale also grew, from 825 million to 980 million. Starting today (November 19), the shares trade on the stock market under the name **ANT**. The company is valued at roughly $1.5 trillion.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **IPO (initial public offering)** | The first time a private company sells shares and lists on a stock exchange |
| **Offering price** | The price at which shares are first sold in an IPO — here, $83 |
| **Fully diluted valuation** | The value of the whole company counting every share that could exist, including ones not yet issued |
| **Greenshoe** | The underwriters' right to buy and sell extra shares for 30 days after an IPO — a way to add supply when demand is strong |
| **Concurrent private placement** | Selling shares separately to large investors at the same price as the IPO — here, suppliers put in $26B |

## One-line definition

Anthropic set its IPO price at **$83** per share on November 18, 2026 — $5 above the top of its $70–78 range — and upsized the offering from 825 million to **980 million** shares. Trading begins November 19 on Nasdaq under the ticker **ANT**, at a fully diluted valuation of about **$1.5T**. This is the confirmed follow-up to yesterday's page ([[concepts/anthropic-ipo-pricing-above-80]]), which left the final price as "unconfirmed."

## Key points

- **Final terms**: $83.00 per share, 980 million Class A shares (about 956.3 million sold by the company + 23,648,646 sold by existing shareholders). Gross proceeds of about $81.3B and a fully diluted valuation of about $1.5T. Lead underwriters Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley.
- **Schedule**: first trading 2026-11-19 on the Nasdaq Global Select Market (ticker ANT); the offering is expected to close 2026-11-20. The underwriters have a 30-day option to buy up to 147 million additional shares (greenshoe).
- **Demand**: per Bloomberg, orders ran to about 7x the offering. Altimeter Capital's Brad Gerstner said he requested a $5B allocation.
- **Watch the numbers — the $107.3B reports**: some outlets report "$107.3B raised, the largest IPO ever, beating SpaceX's $85.7B." That figure adds the $26B concurrent private placement of Class N shares (Google, NVIDIA, AMD, Microsoft, Broadcom, Micron, TSMC, and others, at the IPO price) to the $81.3B public offering. The offering itself is about $81.3B; total capital raised including the placement is about $107.3B. "Largest ever" depends on which basis is used.
- **Financials (same as yesterday)**: annualized revenue run-rate of about $81B (as of October 31, 2026), Q3 2026 revenue of ~$17B (+47% QoQ), multi-year computing and infrastructure commitments of about $900.9B. Founders' Class F shares keep 50.1% of voting power.
- **Confirmation status**: $83 is confirmed by Anthropic's official pricing announcement and dpa-AFX reporting. One outlet noted, however, that as of late evening London time on 11-18, no Form 424B4 prospectus supplement carrying the final price had yet appeared on EDGAR — formal filing confirmation will be possible after 11-19.

## Why it matters (solo-developer lens)

1. **The first frontier lab has gone public**: an AI model company is now on the stock market, which is a turning point in itself. From here, Claude's pricing, investment, and strategy will be judged by quarterly earnings and the share price. For developers who use the models, that means pricing policy may start to move with shareholders watching.
2. **Suppliers become shareholders**: Google, NVIDIA, AMD, Microsoft, and others put in $26B to become shareholders at the same time. Chip sellers and model builders tied together by capital makes Anthropic's computing supply (its $900.9B in commitments) more solid — while also raising independence questions as interests intertwine.
3. **How to read the numbers**: $81.3B and $107.3B are being reported side by side. Without separating the offering, the private placement, and the valuation, it is easy to be misled by "largest ever." The same habit as in [[concepts/llm-evaluation]] applies to reading the news — check the definition of a number first.

## Limits (stated explicitly)

- $83, 980 million shares, and $81.3B are cross-confirmed by Anthropic's official announcement and multiple outlets (dpa-AFX, CNBC, Bloomberg). The final Form 424B4 supplement, however, had not been confirmed on EDGAR at the time of writing, per one outlet.
- "$107.3B, the largest ever" is a total including the private placement; the public offering alone is $81.3B. Which "largest" is meant differs by outlet.
- The first trading price (the open) is different from the $83 offering price. The actual first-day price is set during trading on November 19.
- confidence is medium — an official announcement crossed with multiple reports, with final filing confirmation still pending.
