# Sabi — Baseball-Cap Brain-AI Interface, $50M Seed (2026-10-09)

## One-line definition

Sabi, building a baseball-cap-shaped non-invasive brain-AI interface, raised a $50M seed round led by Khosla Ventures.

## Key points

- **Raise**: $50M seed — Khosla Ventures lead, Accel, Initialized, DST Global, Collaborative Fund, Ascend, and ex-OpenAI CPO Kevin Weil participating.
- **Product**: baseball-cap form factor. Custom EEG chip-embedded sensors (1–5mm) pass through hair to measure neural signals without gel or surgery.
- **Data**: 100,000+ hours of labeled neural recordings — claimed to be the largest public dataset.
- **Chip**: 50x+ power efficiency vs. TI ADS1299 (internal benchmark).
- **Goal**: convert imagined speech and intent into AI commands — communicating with AI without typing or speaking.
- **Timeline**: early prototype at CES 2027.

## Why it matters (pending verification)

The next axis of agent input interfaces is expanding toward **neural signals**. It follows the same pattern as Mecka's motion-data claim ("data is the bottleneck") — Sabi's weapon is a 100,000-hour neural recording dataset. Two takeaways for the AI-native programmer: (1) agent residence may extend from "cloud computers" to "body interfaces," and (2) BCI dataset scale competition could become the next front of model training-data competition. Treat all figures as company claims pending independent verification.
