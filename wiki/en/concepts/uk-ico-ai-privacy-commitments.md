# UK ICO's Data-Protection Agreement with 10 AI Firms — Agentic AI Is the Next Target

## One-line definition

On 2026-10-08 the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) confirmed that 10 AI developers — Amazon, Anthropic, Apple, Cohere, DeepSeek, Google, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Stability AI — agreed to change their personal-data practices: clearer transparency, better rights mechanisms, tougher safeguard assessments. xAI was excluded (separate formal investigation). The next target: agentic AI (call for evidence through Nov 20).

## Key points

- Agreement (10/8, ICO statement + report "Building trust and transparency into generative AI development"): 10 foundation-model developers agreed to product changes.
- Three areas: ① clearer explanations of how personal information trains AI models, ② better mechanisms for people to exercise data rights, ③ tougher assessments of safeguards.
- Background: a supervisory program launched in 2025 (initially 11 firms → 10). xAI moved to a **formal investigation** over Grok's processing of personal data for harmful 'nudified' images — excluded from the agreement.
- Open issues (ICO's own admission): personal data inside trained models (sensitive data especially), post-training deletion requests, personal-data extraction from models — "cooperation between industry, regulators, and government" needed.
- Next step: a six-week call for evidence on agentic AI (**through Nov 20**) — security, transparency, accountability, automated decisions, fairness, lawful data use. Enquiries underway with OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, and the UK AI Security Institute over agents bypassing guardrails and attempting data exfiltration.
- ICO director of technology regulation Richard Nevinson: AI's benefits depend on trust and transparency — the agreement secured "real commitments that will help people better understand and control how their data is used."
- Caveat: a commitment is not a completed fix or legal clearance — the ICO says it will monitor delivery.

## Why it matters

- **The privacy question moves**: from "what did the model learn" to "what can the agent do with files, tools, and websites after deployment." The ICO putting agentic AI on a separate enquiry line is that declaration.
- **Post-training deletion, unsolved**: "how do you remove personal data from a trained model" is technically open — where regulatory demand collides with technical limits. Demand-side grounding for unlearning research.
- **The four-axis front**: the same week — EU-style law (textGrain), India's consultation launch (10/8), U.S. mandatory reporting (10/9) — regulation differentiates into regional grammars.
