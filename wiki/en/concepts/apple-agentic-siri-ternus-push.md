# Apple Wants Siri to Act Like an Agent — CEO Ternus Presses Federighi to Speed Up the AI Roadmap, Target 2027

## Start here

**Analogy**: until now Siri has been a clerk who answers when asked. What Apple wants is a secretary you hand a goal to, who then works it through on their own. The CEO stepping in to say "hurry up" means a clerk can no longer compete.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Agentic assistant** | An AI that remembers a goal and decides its own next steps, continuing without being prompted at every step |
| **Power On** | Bloomberg's Mark Gurman weekly newsletter — a frequent source of Apple insider reporting |
| **App Intents** | Apple's framework that lets apps expose their functions to Siri |

## One-line definition

On 2026-11-08, Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reported in his Power On newsletter that Apple CEO John Ternus pressed software chief Craig Federighi in a recent closed-door meeting to accelerate the AI roadmap beyond incremental Siri improvements. The goal is a Siri that works more like an agent — tracking goals and pursuing them without step-by-step prompts — and Gurman believes it could arrive as early as 2027. Apple has not confirmed any of this.

## Key facts

- **The meeting**: in a closed-door meeting in recent weeks, Ternus told Federighi to "accelerate" the software roadmap. Federighi has historically voiced concerns about the technology's privacy, security, societal and employment implications. Gurman says there is no sign Federighi is leaving. Mike Rockwell, who built Vision Pro and leads Siri engineering, is reportedly still considering stepping down, possibly next year.
- **What an agentic Siri means**: an assistant that holds a goal, decides what to do next, and keeps working without repeated prompts. Gurman pointed to OpenAI's Dots, Meta's Muse and xAI's Grok as examples of agents that track goals and pursue them without step-by-step prompts.
- **Staged timeline**: finer control over Apple's own apps is expected with iOS 27.3 in December (for example, cropping images in Photos or organizing Safari tabs); third-party app integration is expected in spring 2027 (for example, placing a Grubhub order or booking an OpenTable reservation). Siri AI shipped in beta with iOS 27 on September 14, and iOS 27.2 added five languages. On November 3, Apple opened a developer preview letting apps bring their actions into Siri through App Intents.
- **Process change**: Apple is also testing a change to its iOS 28 and macOS 28 development cycles that would give engineers 50% more time to code and explore and less time on integration, because coding agents have improved.

## Why it matters (solo-developer view)

1. **The agent race has reached the OS assistant**: while Dots, Muse and Grok move toward "hand it a goal and it does the work," Siri is still at "ask and it answers." A CEO-level push to make Siri agentic means the definition of an assistant itself is changing.
2. **Privacy worries meet speed**: Federighi's concerns (privacy, security, societal and employment risks) are Apple's long-standing position. An agentic Siri needs deep access to on-device data, so it could be the first case where Apple's privacy principles and agent autonomy collide head-on.
3. **A new distribution channel for developers**: if third-party app integration opens in spring 2027, exposing actions to Siri through App Intents becomes a new entry point — "Siri books the table" changes how users reach an app.

## Limitations (stated)

- Apple has not confirmed the report, which rests on Gurman's sources. AppleInsider noted the account contradicts itself in places and should be taken with a grain of salt.
- Apple delayed promised Siri features once before, in 2025. The 2027 date is Gurman's estimate, not Apple's promise.
- How "agentic" would be implemented (on-device vs cloud, permission scope) is still unknown.
