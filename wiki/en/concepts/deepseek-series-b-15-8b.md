# DeepSeek's Series B — RMB 105.5B Raised, State Share Jumps to 27%

## TL;DR

Details of DeepSeek's second external funding round emerged October 29–30 via The Information's reporting: RMB 105.5 billion (about $15.8 billion) raised at a valuation of about RMB 615.5 billion (about $86 billion), with roughly 30 investors. Tencent and CATL remain the largest private backers, while state-linked investors jumped from about 2% of round one to about 27% of round two.

## Key facts

- **Scale**: RMB 105.5B (about $15.8B), more than double the original roughly RMB 50B target, which was raised mid-process. One of China's largest AI rounds ever. Valuation of about RMB 615.5B (about $86B); some outlets report near $92B on different FX or definitional bases — the yuan figure is primary.
- **Investors**: nearly 30 participants. Tencent contributed RMB 10B in both rounds; CATL and chairman Zeng Yuqun contributed RMB 5B and RMB 10B respectively across the two rounds. New backers include SMIC, ChangXin Memory, vivo, Meituan, Geely, Midea, Luxshare, Ctrip, and Xiaohongshu at RMB 1–3B each.
- **State share**: the China National AI Industry Investment Fund repeated its RMB 1B commitment, and state-owned investors overall rose from about 2% to about 27% of the round.
- **Why the target doubled**: reported reasons are rising prices for high-end Nvidia chips on the black market (an eight-B300 server rising from about $2.06M to $2.61M) and a war chest against AI-bubble worries.
- **Revenue and IPO**: CEO Liang Wenfeng told investors the annualized revenue run rate reached $1B as of September 2026, mostly API revenue — more than double a few months earlier. CITIC Securities was hired to prepare a potential STAR Market IPO (reported in September; no timetable confirmed).

## Why it matters

1. **The low-cost disruptor turns capital-intensive**: the lab famous for cheap models is now competing on compute procurement, not just model efficiency.
2. **Geopolitics meets open weights**: the round closed weeks after the US federal removal order (July), with Chinese industrial and state capital consolidating behind the lab. Open-weight ecosystems are becoming alignment choices, not just technology choices.
3. **Read run rates carefully**: a $1B run rate is an annualized snapshot, not annual revenue. Missing that distinction overstates a lab's financial health.

## Limitations

- Figures come from The Information's reporting and Chinese financial filings, not a company confirmation. The valuation conversion ($86B vs $92B) varies across outlets.
- IPO timetable, size, and target valuation are unconfirmed.
- That a rising state share implies government direction is a reported inference, not an established fact.
