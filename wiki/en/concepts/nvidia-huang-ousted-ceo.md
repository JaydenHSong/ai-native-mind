# Jensen Huang Ousted — NVIDIA Founder-CEO Resigns, Kress Takes Over as Interim (Nov 12)

## Start here

**Analogy**: imagine the lord of a castle he designed and built over 33 years stepping down overnight. The castle still stands, but there is no blueprint for the next lord and no named successor. The share price that day showed the market had been pricing the lord, not the castle.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Interim CEO** | A temporary chief executive until a permanent successor is named — CFO Colette Kress takes the role while keeping her CFO job |
| **Chair** | The person who leads the board of directors — NVIDIA appointed longtime director Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever chair |
| **At-will employment** | An executive employment form with no employment, severance or change-in-control agreements, so leadership can walk away without notice |
| **Prediction market** | A market where people bet money on event probabilities — Polymarket and Kalshi priced Huang's exit by year-end at 75–77% |

## One-line definition

On 2026-11-12, NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang (63) resigned after two extramarital affairs came to light. The board named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO and longtime director Harvey C. Jones as the company's first chair, and began a search for a permanent successor. Shares fell about 8–10% in premarket trading, and more than $500 billion in market value has evaporated since the board opened its investigation.

## Key facts

- **How the resignation happened**: one affair was with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed (Singapore) executive US officials believe works for China's intelligence services; the other was with Kimberly Powell, NVIDIA's senior healthcare executive (the New York Times reported the second affair first). Huang acknowledged infidelity in a staff letter on Sunday (Nov 8–09) and apologised; the board opened an investigation the same weekend. The Wall Street Journal reported on Wednesday (Nov 11) that the board was preparing to oust him, and prediction-market odds of his exit by year-end rose to 75–77%.
- **The new structure**: interim CEO Colette Kress (keeping the CFO role), first chair Harvey C. Jones (longtime director, former Synopsys CEO). The board has begun a search considering internal and external candidates. Jones said the company expects to continue its product roadmap "without interruption."
- **The succession gap**: Huang was a co-founder with roughly 60 direct reports and the largest shareholder (about 3.6%), and had publicly said he does not believe in succession planning. The 2026 proxy states the company is "highly dependent" on its longstanding executive team. Executives are at-will employees with no employment, severance or change-in-control agreements, so leadership can leave without notice.
- **Market reaction**: shares fell about 8% in premarket trading on Thursday (at one point 10% in live coverage), trading near $163 before the bell. More than $500 billion in market value has been wiped out since the investigation began. Citi cut its price target from $285 to $225 (still a buy); Oppenheimer removed its target altogether. Dan Ives (Yorkville) values NVIDIA at $300 with Huang and $225 without him.
- **Geopolitical link**: Megaspeed is under US and Singapore scrutiny over allegations it transshipped export-restricted NVIDIA AI chips to China. Alice Huang has been barred from leaving Singapore, and Taiwan has revoked her entry permit.
- **Schedule**: NVIDIA reports earnings as planned next Tuesday (Nov 17).

## Why it matters (solo-developer view)

1. **The heart of the AI stack just showed governance risk**: NVIDIA is the de facto standard supplier of AI infrastructure. A sudden founder-CEO exit raises questions about chip supply, roadmap and customer continuity. The layer below models and harnesses — silicon — was tied to one person's governance.
2. **No succession plan is itself a price**: the philosophy of "not believing in succession planning" worked for 33 years, but on the day of the exit the market put a $500B+ price on the gap. A solo developer's project has the same structure — a bus factor of 1 eventually gets the same bill.
3. **Earnings on Nov 17 are the next checkpoint**: guidance and roadmap comments in the first earnings under the interim structure are the near-term volatility fork. They land in the same week as Anthropic's IPO pricing decision (Nov 18), concentrating AI-sector news.

## Limitations (stated)

- "Suspected spy" is a US officials' assessment, not an established fact. Alice Huang's connection to an intelligence service has not been officially confirmed.
- The share decline is a premarket figure and differs from intraday trading. The Citi, Oppenheimer and Ives assessments are analyst opinions.
- The Nov 10 daily log excluded "NVDA board probe / Huang" reports as unconfirmed allegations. Today is different: a confirmed resignation announced by the company — the allegation stage and the confirmed event are recorded separately.
