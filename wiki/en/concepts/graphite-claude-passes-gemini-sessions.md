# Graphite Report: Claude Passed Gemini in Chatbot Sessions in June — 5.44B vs 3.72B in September

## Start here

**Analogy**: counting website visits alone, Gemini is second; counting app use as well, Claude is second. Change the measuring stick and the ranking flips. Either way, ChatGPT leads by a double-digit margin.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Session** | One stretch of continuous use — it measures how much and how often people use a service, not how many people use it |
| **Similarweb** | A firm that estimates website and app traffic from a browsing panel |
| **Graphite** | A research and growth-marketing firm that sells SEO and AEO services |

## One-line definition

On 2026-11-09, Graphite published an AI usage report based on Similarweb data, estimating that Anthropic's Claude passed Google's Gemini in monthly chatbot sessions in June 2026 and has stayed ahead every month since. In September, Claude had 5.44 billion sessions against Gemini's 3.72 billion. ChatGPT had 61.35 billion sessions — 81.2% of the six chatbots in the report.

## Key facts

- **The pass and the numbers**: in June, Claude had 4.05 billion sessions to Gemini's 3.75 billion — the first time Claude led. In September, Claude had 5.44 billion (7.2% of about 75.5 billion total), Gemini 3.72 billion, and ChatGPT 61.35 billion (81.2%). The other three — Grok, Perplexity and DeepSeek — each stayed below 10%.
- **Growth rates**: Claude grew 1,901% year over year (from a small base), Gemini 182% and ChatGPT 24%. The six chatbots together reached about 76 billion sessions in September, up 37% year over year. Claude generated 26% of the additional sessions.
- **Methodology and limits**: the counts are Similarweb estimates of web visits plus mobile app sessions. They exclude use through APIs, and Graphite says it has no reliable data source for desktop apps — including Claude's and ChatGPT's — so the count understates desktop use. On web visits alone, Similarweb's August data still put Gemini ahead, with 25.6% of AI chatbot visits to Claude's 9.3% — the report's point is that adding app sessions flips the order.
- **Referral traffic**: in July, ChatGPT accounted for 60% of AI referral traffic, followed by Gemini at 14% and Claude at 10%. The report says AI accounts for less than 1% of traffic to websites on average. Unlike the March edition, this report does not compare AI usage with search engines — the agency says it can no longer reliably separate Google search use from the Google Android app.
- **Timing**: the report came out the same day Anthropic began marketing its IPO. Read with the Menlo report's enterprise API spend (Anthropic 40%, OpenAI 27%, Google 21%), Claude's strength now shows up in consumer app sessions as well.

## Why it matters (solo-developer view)

1. **The benchmark for "most used model" is shifting**: by web visits Gemini is second, but by web plus app sessions Claude is second. Claude's strength sits in coding and work apps; Gemini's in the web and built-in Android use. The model-selection question is moving from "benchmark scores" to "where and how is it used."
2. **ChatGPT's dominance is intact**: an 81.2% share is the bigger number in this report. The fight for second place is heating up while first place stays put.
3. **Read the methodology with the numbers**: Similarweb panel estimates plus a report from Graphite, which sells SEO and AEO services. With API and desktop use excluded, this is a slice of consumer app usage, not all AI use. Together with [[comparisons/consumer-ai-adoption]] (a16z Top 100), the flow is that Claude's consumer adoption moved from third on the web to second including apps.

## Limitations (stated)

- This is coverage of the Graphite report (AIStockWire, Search Engine Journal, Exchange4Media), not the report itself. The figures are Similarweb panel estimates, and Similarweb has revised past numbers.
- API use and desktop apps are excluded, so Claude — whose use skews toward enterprise and developers — may be understated; Gemini's built-in Android use is likewise not fully captured.
- A 1,901% growth rate comes from a small base; read it with the absolute numbers (5.44 billion vs 61.35 billion).
