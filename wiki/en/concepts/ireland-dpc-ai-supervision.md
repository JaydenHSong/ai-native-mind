# Ireland's DPC Delayed or Cancelled 9 AI Launches in Five Years — 180 Products Supervised, 95% Launched Without Delay

## TL;DR

Ireland's Data Protection Commission (DPC) reported in its 75-page report "Responsible Artificial Intelligence Innovation" (published September 25, 2026) that 9 AI product launches were delayed or cancelled after DPC intervention over five years. PPC Land covered the report in detail on November 22. About 180 products were supervised, and 95% launched with no DPC-attributable delay.

## Key facts

- **Supervision scope**: about 180 AI products and services supervised in 2021–2025 (143 generative AI, 37 non-generative). Companies covered include Airbnb, Apple, DeepSeek, Google, LinkedIn, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Pinterest, TikTok, and X.
- **9 delayed or cancelled**: launches delayed or cancelled after DPC intervention — 1 in 2022, 1 in 2023, 4 in 2024, 3 in 2025. Conversely, 95% launched with no delay attributable to DPC involvement.
- **Recommendations**: 59% of products launched in 2024–2025 received recommendations. The median was 3 recommendations per case, and 72% of recommendations concerned transparency.
- **Trend**: AI-related supervision cases grew 10x from 2021 to 2025. In 2025, about a quarter of supervision cases against large tech companies were AI-related.
- **Position**: the DPC said justifying the use of private messages for AI training on "legitimate interests" grounds would be "very difficult to achieve."
- **Date caution**: the report itself was published on September 25, 2026. November 22 is the date of PPC Land's detailed coverage.

## Why it matters

1. **Concrete numbers for GDPR enforcement in practice**: between the claims that "regulation blocks innovation" and that "regulation has no teeth," the actual figures are neither — only 9 launches were delayed or cancelled while 95% went through. That is a usable baseline for pricing regulatory risk into launch schedules.
2. **Transparency is the most common finding**: 72% of recommendations concern transparency. When launching an AI feature, explaining "what, why, and how" is the first thing that gets flagged — designing disclosure and notices transparently from the start is the practical response.
3. **A warning on private-message training**: the DPC drew a line at using private messages for AI training on legitimate-interests grounds. Products that train on chat or message data will face this position directly.

## Limitations (explicit)

- The report itself was published on September 25, 2026; November 22 is the coverage date — do not misread it as a "November 22 report."
- The individual product names behind the 9 delayed or cancelled launches are absent from the coverage summary — the original report is needed for those.
- Figures such as 95%, 59%, and 72% are from the DPC report, cited second-hand via PPC Land coverage.
- Confidence is medium — the DPC report as primary material cross-checked against PPC Land's detailed coverage, but case-level details rely on the coverage summary.
