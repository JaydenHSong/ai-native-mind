# Meta 'Max' Humanoid — a Prototype Built With Fourier in Three Months

## Start here

**Analogy**: a chatbot stepping off the screen. Meta's personal AI agent Muse gaining a body — still at the lab-prototype stage.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Humanoid** | A human-shaped robot designed to work in human environments |
| **Fourier** | A Shanghai robotics company that moved from rehabilitation exoskeletons to the GR-3 humanoid |
| **Jetson Thor** | Nvidia's high-performance compute module for robots |

## One-line definition

Reported by The Information on 2026-10-31 (carried by Reuters): Meta built a humanoid prototype codenamed "Max" with China's Fourier in about three months and has begun a second version. Meta says there are no plans to sell it.

## Key facts

- **Prototype**: codenamed Max, about 106 pounds (48 kg) and 1.5 metres tall, built on Nvidia's Jetson Thor chip. Reportedly has expressive eyes, a moving mouth, and five-fingered hands that can pick up small objects.
- **Process**: a Meta robotics-product executive stayed at Fourier's Shanghai factory for nearly two months during development. Max was demonstrated to employees at an internal robotics event in late August.
- **Second version**: Max 2 has been in development since September, reportedly with improved hands, additional sensors for stairs, and a built-in projector. No launch target exists.
- **Strategic context**: the most concrete result yet from Meta Robotics Studio, founded in early 2025. CTO Andrew Bosworth reportedly described the humanoid internally as Muse's AI agent getting a real body.
- **Official line**: a Meta spokesperson said the collaboration is for internal research with no sales plan, and that sourcing hard-to-obtain parts abroad is an industry-wide reality.

## Why it matters (solo-developer view)

1. **The agent's next form factor is a body**: beyond screens and voice, agents that work in the physical world are the next platform axis. Meta's move confirms the direction.
2. **Speed is the weapon**: a prototype in three months shows how pairing with China's manufacturing ecosystem changes development velocity.
3. **Geopolitics becomes a spec**: a US big-tech lab working with a Chinese robotics firm carries supply-chain and regulatory risk together. Read this as supply-chain news, not just technology news.

## Limitations (stated)

- The evidence is a single sourced report that Reuters explicitly could not independently verify. No public specs, demo, images, or pricing exist.
- Detail figures (height, weight, hand design) vary across secondary coverage and must not be cited as confirmed specs.
- Neither Meta nor Fourier has made a public announcement.
