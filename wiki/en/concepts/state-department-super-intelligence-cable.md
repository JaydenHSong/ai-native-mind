# State Department "Super Intelligence" Wording Order — Department-Level Enforcement of the SI Terminology

## TL;DR

An unclassified 10-19 State Department cable (obtained by Government Executive and Nextgov/FCW) orders all staff to use **"Super Intelligence" (SI)** instead of "artificial intelligence" in new letters, emails, websites, reports, speeches, and policy documents. Existing materials change at their next revision. All official names of governance bodies, systems, platforms, and programs are to be replaced — American AI Exports Program → American SI Exports Program (already reflected on the website). Legal instruments, treaties, contracts, and China's World AI Cooperation Organization are exempt. The first department-level directive implementing Trump's 9-29 executive order, which defines 'Super Intelligence' as the same technology as the existing statutory AI definitions. Verified via Government Executive, Nextgov/FCW, and Defense One.

## Key facts

- **The order**: 10-19 unclassified cable to all staff — "Super Intelligence" (SI) replaces "artificial intelligence" in new documents; existing materials update at next revision.
- **Scope**: official names of all governance bodies, systems, platforms, and programs — American AI Exports Program → American SI Exports Program (website already updated).
- **Exemptions**: legal/treaty/contractual terminology and China's World AI Cooperation Organization keep their names — legally binding language is untouched.
- **Background**: Trump's 9-29 executive order defines SI as the same technology as existing statutory AI definitions — this cable is the first department-level directive executing that EO.
- **Wiki context**: the 10-05~10-13 'Super Intelligence Force' pages covered the federal coordination apparatus; this is a distinct new event — terminology control enforced at department level.

## Why it matters

1. **Terminology is regulatory scope**: the EO defines SI as the same technology as existing statutory AI definitions — the name changes, the scope doesn't. But every future SI-branded rule will stack on this vocabulary. Builders dealing with US government procurement and regulation need the habit of reading "SI" where documents used to say "AI."
2. **The politics of framing**: shifting "AI" to "Super Intelligence" reframes the technology from 'tool' to 'national strategic asset.' The 10-05 Super Intelligence Force (federal coordination) plus the 10-19 State Department wording order show an administration rewriting the very language it governs AI with. Language is the address of budgets and authority.
3. **Limited engineering impact**: the technical definition is unchanged, so no engineering decision changes today — but compliance documents, contracts, and government paperwork need terminology tracking, since "AI"-keyword searches will start missing documents.

## Limitations (explicit)

- A wording order — no change to technical definitions or regulatory substance (the EO defines SI as the same technology).
- State Department only — spread to other departments not yet confirmed.
- Based on reporting of the cable (Government Executive, Nextgov/FCW) — the full cable text is not public.
