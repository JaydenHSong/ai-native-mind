# UK Autumn Budget: Data Centre Covenant — Grid Priority in Exchange for Local Investment

## TL;DR

In his Autumn Budget speech at about 12:30 GMT on 2026-10-28, Chancellor John Healey unveiled a British Data Centre Covenant: operators that invest in local communities, create jobs, and protect water supplies receive priority grid connections and, where necessary, permission to build on-site power generation. The framing is sovereignty and security — keeping sensitive public-service and armed-forces data in Britain as AI use expands — and it turns grid access order itself into an infrastructure incentive.

## Key facts

- **The bargain**: community investment, job creation, and water protection from operators in return for priority grid connections and, where necessary, permission for on-site power generation. With grid queues a bottleneck for AI infrastructure, connection order is itself the incentive.
- **Sovereignty framing**: Healey said he wants to ensure "the British people can benefit from the huge potential upsides of AI," built on sovereignty and security, keeping sensitive data used by public services and the armed forces in Britain.
- **Other AI items in the Budget**: an AI Challenge Prize to encourage AI use in public services, funding to support workers adapting to technological change, and £10M per year to study how AI reshapes jobs and industry (Computer Weekly, pre-Budget announcement).
- **Relation to the pre-Budget package**: the roughly £600M package announced October 24 (£18M Advanced Chip Design Academy, Manchester NCSC AI Cyber Centre of Excellence, £30M locally led AI partnerships, £100M AI Biosecurity Lab cash plus up to £200M in company credits) is confirmed through the Budget. The biosecurity lab was registered in the wiki on 2026-10-25.
- **Budget-wide context**: the Office for Budget Responsibility published its forecasts alongside the speech, extending the forecast horizon to 2031/32 for the first time, against cost-of-living and defence pressures.

## Why it matters

1. **Grid order replaces tax breaks as the attraction tool**: for data-center siting, power, water, and local acceptance now weigh as heavily as chip prices.
2. **Sovereignty becomes a procurement condition**: keeping sensitive data onshore flows down to developers as region choice and data-residency requirements — a policy variable in region strategy.
3. **Pre-announcement vs Budget-day distinction**: the £600M package was pre-announced; the covenant and prize are Budget-day instruments. Separating "already-seen numbers" from "new institutions" avoids duplicate records.

## Limitations

- Enforcement details (investment thresholds, water-protection metrics, sanctions for breach) were not disclosed at speech level.
- The AI Challenge Prize amount and selection process are undisclosed.
- The OBR's AI-related fiscal effects require the full Budget documents; this page covers only the Reuters and Computer Weekly reporting range.
