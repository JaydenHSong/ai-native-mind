# Anthropic–GSA OneGov Expansion: 40% Off Claude for All Levels of Government

## TL;DR

On 2026-10-27 the US General Services Administration expanded its OneGov agreement with Anthropic: government buyers get a 40% discount on token-based usage across Claude models available to the government, with no minimum purchase, and eligibility now extends beyond federal agencies to state, local, tribal, and territorial governments. The discount applies across Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government, and the Claude API — the first time across every product surface.

## Key facts

- **Discount terms**: 40% off token-based usage across Claude models available to the government, including FedRAMP-authorized environments for sensitive unclassified work. No minimum orders; agencies pay only for what they use.
- **Wider eligibility**: federal buyers in the executive, legislative, and judicial branches plus state, local, and tribal governments (GSA's statement; Anthropic's announcement adds territorial governments). Ordering is available directly, through resellers, and on major cloud platforms — cloud-channel discounts arrive in the coming months.
- **Full product surface**: Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government, and the Claude API are all covered for the first time, removing the friction of buying each surface separately.
- **Adoption figures (Anthropic, unverified)**: about 500 US government organizations already use Claude; weekly Claude for Government active users are up about 8x since February, and usage across federal civilian agencies is up about 20x over the past year. Anthropic estimates about $1B in savings from the agreement.
- **What it replaces**: the prior $1-per-user offer expires October 31. GSA says the OneGov strategy has generated more than $1.8B in savings since inception, including about $1.6B through superintelligence agreements. OpenAI switched its OneGov deal to consumption-based pricing last month; Google's $0.47-per-agency Gemini offer expires November 15 (FedScoop).

## Why it matters

1. **Public procurement as a price anchor**: a government price (40% off, consumption-based) becomes a reference point for private-sector price negotiations.
2. **Metering's double edge**: consumption pricing lowers the entry barrier but makes budgets harder to predict — cost governance (caps, monitoring) becomes part of the product for governments and enterprises alike.
3. **From product sales to organization bundles**: following the unified enterprise launch, the all-surface government discount shows Anthropic moving from per-product sales to whole-organization bundles.

## Limitations

- Adoption figures (500 organizations, 8x, 20x) and the $1B savings estimate are Anthropic's own, without independent verification.
- Exact timing for cloud-channel discounts and the per-environment model list were not disclosed.
- The press-release level detail does not show how the discount applies against list prices in each billing path.
