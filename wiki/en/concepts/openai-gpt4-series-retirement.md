# GPT-4 Series Retirement — OpenAI's Generational Turnover

## One-line definition

The bulk retirement of **GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini, GPT-4.1, and GPT-4.1 mini**, notified to API users by official OpenAI email on 2026-10-14 — the formal generational turnover from the GPT-4 series to the GPT-6 line.

## Core content (first-hand: official OpenAI email, "Plan your transition from GPT-4 series to newer OpenAI models")

- **Targets**: GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini, GPT-4.1, GPT-4.1 mini — four models retired in one announcement.
- **Reason** (OpenAI's claim): newer models provide stronger capabilities and safety performance.
- **Form**: a transition notice to API users — a migration advisory. The email's body does not name concrete retirement dates or grace periods (details pending).
- **Position**: the generational turnover GPT-4 series (GPT-4o line) → GPT-6 line (Astra, Sol, etc.) made official. Same current as the GPT-6.1 Sol Ultrafast price-tier polarization (10/11).
- No follow-up press coverage confirmed — single first-party source, cross-check pending.
