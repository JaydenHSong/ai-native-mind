# NVIDIA × Kazakhstan Sovereign AI — 880 GPUs Deployed, 1,000+ Vera Rubin Planned, 45,000-GPU Export License

## TL;DR

On 10-22/23 NVIDIA disclosed Kazakhstan's sovereign-AI buildout: Kazakhtelecom has deployed 400 H200 + 480 B300 GPUs across Astana and Almaty, with 1,000+ more GPUs planned for "Data Center Valley" and 1,000+ NVIDIA Vera Rubin GPUs for the Ekibastuz region in 2027. Jensen Huang visited Astana on 10-23 (closing "AI Month 2026"). Separately, Firebird secured a US export license for 45,000 Grace Blackwell GPUs, with a first Kazakhstan phase targeting commercial launch in Q3 2027. inDrive × Pony.ai will evaluate 7th-gen robotaxis in Astana on NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion + Halos.

## Key facts

- **Deployed**: Kazakhtelecom — 400 H200 + 480 B300 (Astana, Almaty). **Planned for 2027**: 1,000+ GPUs (Data Center Valley), 1,000+ Vera Rubin (Ekibastuz).
- **Huang's visit**: 10-23 Astana "AI Month 2026" — fireside chat with Deputy PM and AI minister Zhaslan Madiyev; pitch of Kazakhstan as a European-facing AI compute hub (energy, connectivity, national LLM).
- **Firebird**: US export license for 45,000 Grace Blackwell GPUs; first Kazakhstan phase (with Kazakhtelecom, Data Center Valley) targets Q3 2027 commercial launch (~500 MW total potential); Firebird Labs Kazakhstan for local talent.
- **Robotaxis**: inDrive × Pony.ai to evaluate 7th-gen robotaxis in Astana (NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion + Halos, DRIVE AGX Thor, up to 4,000 FP4 TFLOPS).
- **Backdrop**: June 2026 Kazakhstan signed ~$10B in AI/digital-infrastructure agreements; government target: a 100,000-GPU Data Center Valley cluster, $3B+/yr export revenue, live in 2027.

## Why it matters

1. **Compute geography is diversifying**: the execution base for models and agents is spreading beyond the US–China duopoly. More "where to run" options is a price-and-access variable even for solo developers.
2. **Vera Rubin moves from roadmap to deployment plans**: the next-gen platform now appears in actual national deployment plans — a signal for reading hardware-generation turnover.
3. **The export-license variable**: a 45,000-unit license shows "who gets the chips" is the new front of the chip war — paired on the same day with Lava's B300 leakage tracing: the legal route (licenses) and the illicit route (smuggling) making news together.

## Limitations (explicit)

- Deployment figures and 2027 plans come from NVIDIA's blog and press coverage — realization is unconfirmed.
- Specific conditions of Firebird's 45,000-unit license (end-user restrictions etc.) are undisclosed.
- The inDrive × Pony.ai robotaxis are in "evaluation" — no commercial timeline.
