# Gemini 4 'Carbon' Internal Testing — Opus 5.5-Level Coding Assessment

## One-line definition

An internal Gemini 4-family variant 'Carbon' being tested on Google's internal coding platform 'Jetski' — employees assess its coding performance at Anthropic Opus 5.5 level (Business Insider 10/9; Google declined to comment).

## Key points

- Testing: the Gemini 4-family internal variant 'Carbon' under test on Google's internal coding platform Jetski (Business Insider, 10/9).
- Employee assessment: coding performance "feels like Opus 5.5" — another employee called it "Carbon is really good." In effect, internal acknowledgment of Opus 5.5-level coding.
- Three checkpoints: internal documents show **Argon, Barium, and Carbon under parallel testing**. Barium-B is reportedly selected as the upcoming public Argon release.
- Whether Carbon is a separate model or an Argon update is unconfirmed. Google declined to comment.
- Evidence limit: single original report by Business Insider (cited by the-decoder, storyboard18, aistockwire, testingcatalog) — treat as reference-grade.

## Why it matters

- **The Gemini 4 family strategy, revealed**: behind the public flagship Argon run Barium (upcoming public version) and Carbon (internal coding-focused) in parallel — "one model" is actually a checkpoint portfolio.
- **The internal version of the benchmark gap**: [[concepts/gemini-4-argon]] showed self-reported benchmarks (12/18 leading) vs the independent index (AA 53). Carbon's "Opus 5.5-level" is an employee-felt assessment — a claim until independently verified.
- **Performance war, not price war**: while Anthropic pushes a 90% price cut with Haiku 5.5 the same week, Google answers with coding performance — the frontier race's two fronts.
