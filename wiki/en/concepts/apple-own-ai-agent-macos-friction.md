# Apple Is Building Its Own AI Agent — Job Posting Deleted, macOS 27.3 Makes Rival Agent Approval 14 Steps (Nov 19–20)

## Start here

**Analogy**: Apple — which had made it hard to use other companies' AI assistants on its computers — is now building its own AI assistant. It posted a job ad, then quietly deleted it a day later, and at the same time a new macOS beta stretches the process of turning on a rival AI assistant from 4 steps to 14. It is a plain display of platform power: "we set the rules in our house."

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Computer-use agent** | An AI that uses a computer on a person's behalf — looking at the screen, clicking, and operating apps |
| **Third-party** | A company other than Apple — here, rival agent makers such as Anthropic and OpenAI |
| **DOJ suit** | The US Department of Justice's antitrust suit against Apple, in which blocking third-party access is an issue |

## One-line definition

According to a November 19, 2026 report by Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, Apple is developing an autonomous AI agent that browses the web, controls apps, and writes and debugs code. The evidence is a 'Senior Engineering Manager, Agentic AI – Proactive' job posting published that week. Apple deleted the posting on November 20. At the same time, the macOS 27.3 beta is making approval for outside AI agents more demanding.

## Key points

- **The agent's shape**: a proactive, general-purpose agent that can "use a computer the way a capable person does" — web browsing, cross-app work, running commands, writing and debugging code, calling services, and orchestrating specialist sub-agents. It works with the Siri, UI Frameworks, and Apple Foundation Models teams.
- **Timeline**: launch is targeted for as early as the end of 2027, but development is at an early stage. Apple declined to comment.
- **Organization**: new CEO John Ternus, who took office September 1, 2026, is pressing software chief Craig Federighi to accelerate development (per Techstrong.ai). This continues [[concepts/apple-agentic-siri-ternus-push]].
- **Market reaction**: Anthropic fell 4.7% in after-hours trading ($92.60) right after the report; Meta fell about 2%.
- **Posting deleted**: Apple removed the job posting on November 20.
- **macOS 27.3 beta**: consent warnings and approval steps for outside AI agents are being tightened. Splitting Device Control and Data Access settings stretches enabling ChatGPT computer-use support from 4 steps to 14. Rivals read it as protecting Apple's own turf, and a DOJ suit over blocking third-party access is pending.

## Why it matters (solo-developer lens)

1. **The last big platform without its own agent is entering**: once Apple has one, the question of how a platform controls the distribution of third-party agents such as Claude and Muse becomes real. The "who gets in" question in [[concepts/websites-block-ai-agents]] moves from the website level to the operating-system level.
2. **Friction itself is the strategy**: without blocking anything outright, stretching the procedure to 14 steps slows rival-agent adoption while driving no users away. "Making it annoying" is a quieter form of platform power than "blocking."
3. **The DOJ suit sits in the background**: Apple is tightening its approval procedure even while a suit over third-party access blocking is pending. Whether this friction stays or loosens will turn on the suit's outcome.

## Limits (stated explicitly)

- The agent's development rests on a Bloomberg report based on a job posting; Apple has not officially confirmed it. The launch target (end of 2027) is a goal, not a confirmed schedule.
- The reason the posting was deleted is unconfirmed — it could be a completed hire, a strategy change, or a reaction to the coverage.
- The 14-step procedure is a beta figure and could change by the official release.
- confidence is medium — Bloomberg and TechCrunch cross-check, but Apple has not confirmed and the work is early-stage.
