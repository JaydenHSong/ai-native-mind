# Italy's Legislative Decree 179/2026 Takes Effect — First EU State to Put AI Safety in Occupational-Safety Docs

## TL;DR

Italy's Legislative Decree No. 179 (adopted 10-07, published in Gazzetta Ufficiale n. 234 on 10-08) took effect on 2026-10-23 with no transition period — the first EU member state to embed AI safety obligations directly into national occupational-safety documentation (DVR). Article 41: final decisions on hiring, contract changes, dismissal, discipline, or performance ratings may not rest solely on automated processing — a natural person must decide, and dismissals in breach are void. Supervision by AgID (conformity) and ACN (market surveillance, Brussels contact point); fines up to €35M or 7% of worldwide turnover.

## Key facts

- **Timeline**: adopted 10-07 by the President of the Republic → published 10-08 → in force 10-23 (no transition). 54 articles; implements Article 24 of Law 132/2025 to align with the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).
- **Article 41 (Chapter III)**: no "solely automated" final decisions on hiring, contract changes, dismissal, discipline, or performance ratings — a human must decide. Breach dismissals are void. Workers can demand an explanation of the AI's weight and main parameters.
- **DVR obligation**: the mandatory workplace risk document (Documento di Valutazione dei Rischi) must assess AI — algorithmic scheduling, work-organization AI, sensor-equipped PPE, predictive maintenance — including technostress and hallucination-driven errors.
- **Supervisors**: AgID (digital agency, conformity bodies) + ACN (National Cybersecurity Agency, market surveillance, Brussels contact point). Creates Italy's AI regulatory sandbox.
- **Fines**: up to €35M or 7% of worldwide turnover.

## Why it matters

1. **Enforcement channel shift**: from "tech regulation" to "labor inspection." Any enterprise customer deploying agents that schedule, monitor, or evaluate workers now faces a new compliance question — a sales barrier and a design requirement for B2B agent products.
2. **The human-decision gate, legislated**: solely-automated-decision ban + void dismissals + explanation rights. Where Duet's approval gate was UX, here the "human approval gate" is statutory in one EU state — same design problem, legal force.
3. **Precedent effect**: the first EU case. The "DVR-embedding" model may be cited in other member states' implementing laws — agent products targeting the EU market should watch this pattern.

## Limitations (explicit)

- Day-one reporting — no enforcement cases or supervisory guidance yet.
- The scope of "final decision" in Article 41 (where recommendation-level AI ends) awaits case law and interpretation.
- Regulatory-sandbox operating details undisclosed.
