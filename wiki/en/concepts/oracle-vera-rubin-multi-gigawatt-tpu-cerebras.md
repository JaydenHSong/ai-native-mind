# Oracle Expands Vera Rubin to Multi-Gigawatt Scale, Adds Google TPUs and Cerebras

## TL;DR

At Oracle AI World in Las Vegas on 2026-10-27, Oracle said it will expand to multi-gigawatt scale with Nvidia's newest Vera Rubin AI servers, without disclosing the deal's value, while also offering Google TPUs for the first time and adding Cerebras and SambaNova chips — all positioned for inference, with Nvidia remaining the training core. Even Nvidia-based systems will use some of Oracle's in-house Acceleron networking chips instead of Nvidia networking.

## Key facts

- **Vera Rubin scale-up**: Oracle Cloud Infrastructure expands to multi-gigawatt scale with Vera Rubin AI servers; value undisclosed. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has previously said Vera Rubin systems can generate up to $40B in revenue per gigawatt for Nvidia.
- **OCI availability**: Vera Rubin NVL72 systems and Vera CPUs are generally available on OCI; HGX Rubin NVL8 systems with eight Rubin GPUs are planned for frontier training and agentic workloads (MarketFlux).
- **In-house networking**: Oracle will use some of its own Acceleron networking chips in place of certain Nvidia networking components, even on Nvidia-based systems — extending vertical integration beyond accelerators.
- **The diversification**: Google TPUs come to OCI for the first time — the first hyperscaler outside Google to offer them — with TPU 8t for training and TPU 8i for inference, starting in 2027. Cerebras high-speed inference and SambaNova's SambaRack SN50 inference chips also arrive in 2027.
- **Market reaction**: Oracle shares rose roughly 4–5% on the day to around $132 (MarketFlux). Cerebras CEO Andrew Feldman framed the deal as deployment "in other people's data centers at large scale," expanding its tier-one customer base.

## Why it matters

1. **Inference goes multi-polar**: training stays close to a Nvidia monopoly, but inference chip choice is widening — Oracle's shelf expansion signals inference unit costs may fall with supplier competition.
2. **Cloud selection criteria shift**: from "which GPUs does it have" to "which chip combinations can I choose" — workload-matched silicon becomes a cost-optimization tool.
3. **Networking as the next integration layer**: Oracle's Acceleron push means benchmark numbers must be read alongside the silicon composition behind them.

## Limitations

- Deal value, gigawatt count, and delivery timing were not disclosed, so the expansion cannot be quantified.
- Google TPU, Cerebras, and SambaNova availability is slated for 2027, not immediately usable.
- The share-price move (about 4–5%) is a MarketFlux report and is not separated from broader market movement that day.
