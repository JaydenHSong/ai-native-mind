# OpenAI Cuts Funding Target to $20B — Valuation Holds at $1.25T, First Close Expected Short

## TL;DR

The Financial Times reported October 30, 2026 (carried by Reuters) that OpenAI cut its latest funding target by $10 billion, to about $20 billion, at a $1.25 trillion valuation. The valuation had already dropped from $1.4 trillion on October 22; this time the amount followed. The first close is expected below $20 billion, and the Wall Street Journal put commitments at about $11 billion — a conflict this page preserves rather than resolves.

## Key facts

- **The shrinking trajectory**: late September, $30B at $1.4T → October 22, $30B at $1.25T (Bloomberg, CNBC) → October 30, about $20B at $1.25T (FT). The target fell by a third in five weeks.
- **First-close conflict**: the FT says the first close is expected below $20 billion after some investors trimmed allocations; the WSJ reported about $11 billion committed, under half the original target. Report both figures; neither is a confirmed close.
- **Expected participants**: a UAE consortium including state-backed MGX is expected to be the largest participant, with OpenAI gaining an option to lease newly built computing capacity in the region as part of the deal. SoftBank, Sequoia, Coatue, Founders Fund, and Andreessen Horowitz are also expected to participate.
- **Retail tranche**: a subsequent offering for wealthy individuals at the same $1.25 trillion valuation is under consideration.
- **Competitive context**: the cut lands amid a tech selloff and a race against Anthropic's expected mid-November IPO (S-1 public October 26) for the same investor pool. OpenAI is reported to be pursuing a listing in 2027.

## Why it matters

1. **Even frontier labs cannot set their price**: the amount moved before the valuation did — the funding cycle for AI infrastructure is turning at the quantity margin first. That is the most direct repricing signal of the cycle.
2. **Compute is the deal term**: MGX's participation comes with a capacity-lease option. Money is no longer the scarce term; secured compute is.
3. **An IPO race, not a solo round**: Anthropic's listing competes for the same capital. One lab's shrinking round changes the other's listing conditions.

## Limitations

- The FT story is paywalled; the facts here come via Reuters and WSJ summaries. OpenAI did not respond to Reuters' request for comment.
- The only round OpenAI itself has confirmed is the March close ($122B at an $852B post-money valuation).
- The first-close size differs sharply between the FT and WSJ reports.
