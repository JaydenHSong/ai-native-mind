# GenRobot AI's $100M Series A+ — Human-Demonstration Data Infrastructure for Robots

## Start here

**Analogy**: teaching a robot a job needs a textbook of 'people doing the job on video', not a manual. GenRobot sells the filming and editing of that textbook.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Embodied AI data** | Demonstration and sensor data for training physical robot behavior |
| **Series A+** | A follow-on round after Series A |

## One-line definition

Disclosed 2026-11-02: Beijing-based GenRobot AI raised a $100M Series A+ (closed in Q3) for robot training-data infrastructure, with SAIC and Baidu among the backers.

## Key facts

- **Product**: GenRobot does not build robots. It sells capture rigs and software that record people doing household, manufacturing and logistics tasks — visual information plus hand and body movements — and its Matrix platform synchronizes, cleans and labels the recordings into training and evaluation data.
- **Investors**: strategic backers SAIC Motor, Baidu, Eastworlds, Shoucheng Holdings and Galaxy General Robotics; financial backers GL Ventures, CITIC Goldstone, CCB International and Huatai Zijin. Valuation and use of funds were not disclosed.
- **Scale (company and press figures)**: more than 30 AI-company partners, more than one million hours of data held. Quarterly effective data delivered rose 10x, from 100,000 hours to one million hours, with a plan for 10 million hours in a single quarter in 2027, per PEdaily. An open-source dataset of 13,000+ hours released earlier this year is said to draw over one million downloads a month.
- **Background**: founded May 2025. By August, Gasgoo reported it had already raised over $200M across seven consecutive rounds. Whether this A+ is included in that total is not stated.

## Why it matters (solo-developer view)

1. **The robotics bottleneck moved from hardware to data**: after Mecka's $60M, nine-figure funding is flowing into data infrastructure.
2. **Data is also evaluation**: GenRobot sells evaluation data alongside training data, and reproducible evaluation data is becoming the basis for choosing robot models.
3. **China's funding stack is thick**: automakers, a search giant and robot makers investing together treats robot data as industrial infrastructure.

## Limitations (stated)

- Scale figures come from company claims via Chinese press reports, without independent verification.
- Valuation and use of funds are undisclosed, and whether the A+ is inside the earlier $200M+ total is unclear.
