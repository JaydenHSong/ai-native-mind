# NVIDIA's Autonomy Stack — Two OEMs Converging on Hyperion, Alpamayo, and Halos

## Start here

**Analogy**: just as smartphones standardized on "Qualcomm chips, Android OS," cars are standardizing on "Mercedes-Benz and Volvo bodies, NVIDIA brains." Two automakers adopted the same brain stack a day apart.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Hyperion** | In-vehicle compute + sensor stack |
| **Alpamayo** | Full-stack automated-driving software with AI reasoning — Alpamayo 2, the open reasoning driving model, opened in August 2026 |
| **Halos** | Parallel safety stack |
| **Eyes-off / mind-off** | Automation at the level where the driver looks away and thinks elsewhere (L3–L4 orientation) |

## One-line definition

The case of Mercedes-Benz and Volvo Cars announcing expanded adoption of NVIDIA's three-layer automated-driving stack (Hyperion, Alpamayo, Halos) a day apart — **physical-AI platform convergence by OEMs**, shown not as numbers but as contracts.

## Key points

- **Mercedes-Benz** (2026-10-16, NVIDIA newsroom + Mercedes press release): expanded strategic partnership. Hyperion integrated into MB.OS. Targets: urban point-to-point assistance, automated parking, active safety, and future SAE L4 'robolimousines'.
- **Quote**: Mercedes CTO Jörg Burzer — 'NVIDIA is our most important strategic partner in automated driving'.
- **Volvo Cars**: expanded a partnership running since 2017 (separate announcement). Debut in the next-generation model, eyes-off/mind-off target. CEO Håkan Samuelsson.
- **Mercedes' wider NVIDIA use**: simulation and the MO360 production system.
- **Alpamayo 2**: the open reasoning driving model, opened August 2026.
- **Undisclosed**: launch dates, vehicles, financial terms — the scope of the announcement.

## Why it matters (solo-developer lens)

1. **Platform concentration in physical AI**: AMD's $8.2B World Labs acquisition ([[concepts/amd-world-labs-physical-ai]]) and NVIDIA's two axes — "world models vs driving stack" now visible through OEM contracts.
2. **Reasoning driving models = the agent paradigm, transplanted**: Alpamayo applies the agent structure (judge → act) to driving — driving reads as a harness problem.
3. **The vendor lock-in question**: two global OEMs converging on one stack → "the car is hardware, the stack is NVIDIA" as vertical separation. From chips ([[concepts/microsoft-maia-300]]) to stacks — pricing power's axis moves one level up.

## Limits (stated explicitly)

- Launch dates, vehicles, and financial terms undisclosed — the announcement's declarative character.
- Collected via secondhand outlets (tbreak, europesays) — primary verification from the NVIDIA newsroom still needed.
- Alpamayo 2's real performance and safety record are undisclosed.
