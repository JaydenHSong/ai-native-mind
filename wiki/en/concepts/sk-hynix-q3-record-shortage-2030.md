# SK hynix Posts Record Q3 — Memory Shortage Expected Through 2030

## TL;DR

SK hynix's third-quarter 2026 results, announced Wednesday 2026-10-28, were the biggest in its history: revenue of 104.62 trillion won (about $78.1B), up 328% year over year, with an 80% operating margin — a company record. The headline that matters more than the quarter is the outlook: the company says the structure in which demand exceeds supply will persist through 2030, as leading-edge process difficulty and long fab lead times keep supply constrained.

## Key facts

- **Results**: revenue 104.62 trillion won (about $78.1B), +328% year over year and +32% quarter over quarter; operating margin 80% (up 3 points sequentially, vs 78.4% consensus). Net profit 39.78 trillion won (about $29.7B), +216% year over year but -58% sequentially — the prior quarter included a large one-off Kioxia investment gain.
- **Prices**: DRAM average selling prices rose by a high-20% range quarter over quarter, NAND by a mid-20% range. Price rises plus a richer product mix drove the quarter.
- **Why supply stays short**: the technical difficulty of leading-edge processes and the long lead time to bring new fabs online, with physical AI (autonomous driving, robotics) adding demand. The company expects demand to exceed supply through 2030.
- **The future is already contracted**: more than 50% of 2027 and 2028 output is covered by long-term contracts, with about five-year deals signed with 14 major customers. The M15X fab ramps to maximum capacity by year-end; the Yongin cluster's Phase 1 enters full-scale operation next year.
- **HBM4 and shareholder returns**: next-generation HBM4 is expected to exceed HBM3E in shipments and revenue in Q4 (TokenPost). The 2026 shareholder return program is worth about $75.4B (dividends plus buybacks and cancellations).

## Why it matters

1. **Memory is the second AI bottleneck**: after GPU prices, memory price and availability now feed directly into cloud and inference costs — infrastructure cost analysis must track the memory cycle too.
2. **Contracted supply squeezes small players**: with over half of two years' output pre-sold, spot-market supply gets scarcer and harder for smaller buyers to secure.
3. **Duration matters more than the quarter**: even record results left secondary share-price reports conflicting, because the market is pricing how long the cycle lasts, not how good one quarter was.

## Limitations

- Won/dollar figures vary in presentation across secondaries (104.62 trillion vs 10.462 trillion won renderings); dollar figures (about $78.1B revenue, about $29.7B net profit) are used here for consistency.
- Secondary reports conflicted on the share-price move, so no direction is recorded.
- The through-2030 shortage outlook is the company's own forecast and depends on fab ramp speed and demand trends.
